Cairo comes alive after dark. Dinner is eaten late, the old city glows under its lamps, and the Nile fills with lit-up boats. If your days are spent at the pyramids and museums, the evenings are when you meet the city itself. Here are the best ways to spend them.

A Nile dinner cruise

The classic Cairo evening: dinner on a restaurant boat while it cruises past the lights of the city, with live music and usually a show. The highlight for many is the tanoura, the spinning dance with multicoloured skirts, related to Sufi whirling. Many boats also have a belly dance show. See our Nile dinner cruise.

Choosing a boat

Check whether drinks are included, which shows are on, where the boat departs and whether hotel transfers are included.

A street-food tour

Cairo's food is best on the street and in small local restaurants: koshari, ful medames, ta'meya, feteer and hawawshi. An evening food tour with a local guide takes you through the backstreets and explains what you're eating. See our Cairo street food tour by night.

The old city after dark

  • Al-Muizz Street: the main street of Fatimid Cairo, lined with mosques, madrasas and fountains, and lively in the evening.
  • Khan el-Khalili: the old bazaar, with lanes of shops and traditional cafés for tea and a water pipe.
  • Al-Azhar Park: a hilltop garden above the old city, the place to watch the sunset before heading down to the lanes.

Our Cairo guide has a walking route through the old city.

A felucca at sunset

For something quieter than a dinner cruise, hire a felucca from the Corniche for an hour on the river at sunset. Agree the time and price before you board.

Tips for Cairo evenings

  • Dinner is late. Many places stay busy past midnight.
  • Plan your evening by area. Traffic can make crossing the city slow.
  • Dress modestly in the old city, and carry small notes for tea and tips.
  • Ride-hailing apps are the easiest way to get around at night.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Nile dinner cruise worth it?

For an easy, festive evening with a view, yes. If you prefer quiet, a felucca at sunset suits you better.

Is the old city safe at night?

Al-Muizz Street and Khan el-Khalili are busy, lived-in areas in the evening. As in any big city, watch your bag and phone.

Can I do a food tour and a cruise on the same night?

They overlap in time, so choose one per evening. With two nights in Cairo, do both.
