Cairo has three great museums of ancient Egypt, and in the last few years their collections have moved between them. The royal mummies left Tahrir in 2021, and Tutankhamun's treasures went to Giza. This guide explains what each museum holds now, so you can choose.

The Egyptian Museum in Tahrir

The Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square, designed by the architect Marcel Dourgnon, opened on 15 November 1902. For more than a century it held the world's largest collection of Egyptian antiquities, and it is still a museum of masterpieces:

  • The Narmer Palette, from the Early Dynastic period.
  • Old Kingdom statues of the kings Djoser, Khafre and Menkaure.
  • The New Kingdom: a statue of Hatshepsut and the Merneptah Stele.
  • Akhenaten and the Amarna period, including a bust of the king.
  • The objects of Yuya and Thuya, shown on the upper floor since the 2018 reorganisation.
  • The treasures of Tanis and the masks of Late Period kings.

Two things have left: the royal mummies, now at the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization, and the objects from Tutankhamun's tomb, which went to the Grand Egyptian Museum.

The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization

The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC) stands in Fustat, in Old Cairo, beside the Ain el-Sira lake. It opened in part in 2017 and was inaugurated on 3 April 2021, the day of the Pharaohs' Golden Parade: 22 royal mummies, 18 kings and 4 queens, among them Ramesses II and Hatshepsut, were carried through the city from the Egyptian Museum.

  • The Royal Mummies Hall is the reason most visitors come.
  • The main galleries tell the story of Egypt from prehistory to the modern era, with around 50,000 objects, by period and by theme: the Nile, writing, state and society, beliefs.
  • The textile gallery shows about 600 pieces.

The nearest metro station is Mar Girgis on Line 1, the same station as Coptic Cairo, so the two fit well into one day.

The Grand Egyptian Museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum, in Giza near the pyramids, opened in November 2025. It brings together objects from across Egypt, and for the first time it shows the full collection from the tomb of Tutankhamun together. Read our guide to the Grand Egyptian Museum.

Which one should you choose?

  • One museum, first trip: the Grand Egyptian Museum, for Tutankhamun, and it pairs with the pyramids in one day.
  • The pharaohs themselves: the NMEC, for the royal mummies.
  • Classic masterpieces and the old museum's atmosphere: the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir, easy to reach from downtown.
  • Time for two: the Grand Egyptian Museum with the pyramids one day, and the NMEC with Coptic Cairo another.

Before you go

Collections are still being moved and rearranged. Check what is on display and the opening times on each museum's official channels before your visit.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Tutankhamun's mask now?

The objects from Tutankhamun's tomb have moved from the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir to the Grand Egyptian Museum in Giza.

Where can I see the royal mummies?

At the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization in Fustat. They were moved there from the Egyptian Museum on 3 April 2021.

Is the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir still worth a visit?

Yes, if you have time: it still shows masterpieces such as the Narmer Palette, the royal statues of the Old Kingdom and the Amarna collection.
