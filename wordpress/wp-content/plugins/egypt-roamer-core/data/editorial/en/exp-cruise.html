What it is

Between Aswan and Luxor, the Nile passes some of the best-preserved temples in Egypt. On a cruise the boat is your hotel and moves between the sites: your cabin, meals and the visits are arranged together, and a guide takes you to the temples at each stop. Between visits there is time on deck to watch the river: palm groves, villages and desert on either side.

On the way

  • Kom Ombo. A double temple of the Ptolemaic and Roman periods on a bend of the river, dedicated to two gods at once: Sobek, the crocodile god, and Horus, the falcon-headed god.
  • Edfu. The Temple of Horus at Edfu is one of the best-preserved temples in Egypt. It was begun under Ptolemy III in 237 BC and completed in 57 BC, and its simple plan along one main axis is the classic example of an Egyptian temple.
  • Luxor. The cruise ends in Luxor, with its temples on the East Bank and the royal tombs on the West Bank.

Before you book

The options on this page sail from Aswan to Luxor. They differ in what is added: some include a trip to Abu Simbel and a hot-air balloon flight over Luxor, others offer them as extras, and entrance fees are included on some and paid separately on others. Each option below lists what it includes. Check what is included on the partner's page before you book.

Good to know

  • Abu Simbel and balloon flights start very early in the morning.
  • Abu Simbel lies far south of Aswan and is visited as a separate trip from the city.
  • Photography rules inside the temples can change. Follow the guards.

Who it suits

Choose a cruise if you want an easy, relaxed trip with the river as the highlight: you unpack once, and the slow days on deck are, for many travellers, the best memory of Egypt. If the monuments are your priority and you like to set your own pace, staying in hotels may suit you better. Our guide Nile Cruise or Hotels? explains the difference.

Combine it with

Many people add an extra day in Luxor before or after the cruise for the sites the boat schedule rushes, such as the Valley of the Kings.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to start in Luxor or in Aswan?

Both work. Starting in Aswan ends the trip in Luxor, which has more to see afterwards. The options on this page all sail from Aswan to Luxor.

Does the cruise include Abu Simbel?

On some options, yes, as an early-morning trip from Aswan. On others it is an extra. Each option says which.

Are the temple visits guided?

On the options on this page, yes: an Egyptologist guide leads the visits. Check what is included when you book.
