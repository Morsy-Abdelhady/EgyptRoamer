What it is

A day trip from Aswan to Abu Simbel, far to the south near the Sudanese border. You'll see the two rock-cut temples built by Ramesses II: the Great Temple, fronted by four colossal seated statues of the king, and the smaller temple dedicated to Queen Nefertari and the goddess Hathor. Both belong to the UNESCO site Nubian Monuments from Abu Simbel to Philae.

Why it's worth the effort

  • Scale. The façade of the Great Temple is one of the most famous images of ancient Egypt, and it's even bigger in person.
  • The rescue. In the 1960s, as Lake Nasser rose behind the new High Dam, the temples were cut into blocks and reassembled on higher ground. Knowing that as you stand inside makes the visit remarkable.
  • The interiors. Carved halls lead deep into the rock, with battle scenes and statues of the king as a god.

Getting there: road or air

  • By road: the classic option, leaving Aswan in the dark before dawn and arriving in the morning. It's a very long day, most of it in the vehicle.
  • By air: short flights from Aswan save hours on the road, at a higher cost.

Whichever you choose, the time at the temples themselves is usually a few hours.

Plan the day

  • Bring breakfast or snacks, water, and a layer for the early morning.
  • Sun protection is essential. The forecourt is fully exposed.
  • Photography rules inside the temples can change. Follow the guards.

The sun festival

On or around 22 February and 22 October, the rising sun shines along the Great Temple's axis into the innermost sanctuary and lights the seated statues there. These days draw large crowds and events. Book far ahead if you want to see it.

Who it suits

Anyone with a real interest in ancient Egypt, and travellers staying at least two days in Aswan. If long road journeys are hard for you, fly, or consider a Lake Nasser cruise, which reaches Abu Simbel by water.

Combine it with

Philae temple, the Unfinished Obelisk and a felucca in Aswan. Plan them for the days before or after, not the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit Abu Simbel without a tour?

Yes, you can arrange your own driver or fly independently. Most travellers still book a tour or transfer for the pre-dawn road journey.

How long do you spend at the temples?

Usually a few hours, enough for both temples without rushing.

Is it worth going on the sun festival day?

It's unforgettable, but very busy. On other days you get far more space in the temples.
