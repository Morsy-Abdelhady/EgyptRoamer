Lake Burullus lies in Kafr El Sheikh Governorate. It is the second-largest natural lake in the country, and its wetlands receive migratory birds. This guide is based on the facts given by Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency, which manages the country's protected areas.

The protected area in brief

  • Declared: 1998.
  • Area: 484 km².
  • Type: protected area for the sustainable use of natural resources.
  • Where: Kafr El Sheikh Governorate, about 300 km from Cairo.

The lake and its shores

By area, Lake Burullus is the second-largest natural lake in Egypt. The Agency lists several environments around it:

  • Salt marshes and reed marshes.
  • Sandy plains.
  • High sand dunes along the shores of the lake.

Each has its own kind of soil, which shapes what grows there. Together they are a natural home for about 135 kinds of wild and water plants.

Birds

The wetlands play a large part in receiving wild migratory birds.

How to visit

  • Protect the place: leave no rubbish, and keep your distance from the birds.
  • Entry fees and rules: they can change, so check with the Environmental Affairs Agency before you go.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Lake Burullus?

In Kafr El Sheikh Governorate, about 300 km from Cairo.

How big is Lake Burullus?

It is the second-largest natural lake in Egypt by area. The protected area covers 484 km².

Why is Lake Burullus protected?

For its salt and reed marshes, sandy plains and dunes, about 135 kinds of wild and water plants, and its wetlands, which receive migratory birds.

When was Lake Burullus declared a protected area?

In 1998.
