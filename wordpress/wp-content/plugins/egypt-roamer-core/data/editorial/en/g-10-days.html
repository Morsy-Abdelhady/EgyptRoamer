Ten days lets you add something the seven-day itinerary has to leave out: the sea. This plan covers the great sites of the Nile Valley, then ends with three days on the Red Sea to rest, snorkel or dive. To compare the 7, 10 and 14-day plans, see how to plan your Egypt itinerary.

The plan at a glance

  • Days 1–3: Cairo. The pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Islamic and Coptic Cairo.
  • Days 4–5: Aswan. Philae, the Nile by felucca, and Abu Simbel.
  • Days 6–7: Luxor. The East Bank temples and the West Bank tombs.
  • Days 8–10: Hurghada. Reefs, islands and rest.

Fly from Cairo to Aswan, travel north to Luxor by road, train or boat, cross to Hurghada by road, and fly home from Hurghada.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 — Arrive in Cairo Settle in and have an easy first evening.
  2. Day 2 — Giza The pyramids and the Sphinx early, then the Grand Egyptian Museum.
  3. Day 3 — Old Cairo Coptic Cairo and Islamic Cairo, with an evening on Al-Muizz Street. See Cairo at night.
  4. Day 4 — Aswan Fly south. Philae in the afternoon and a felucca at sunset.
  5. Day 5 — Abu Simbel A pre-dawn start for Abu Simbel, back in Aswan by the afternoon.
  6. Day 6 — To Luxor North by road, stopping at Kom Ombo and Edfu. Luxor Temple at dusk.
  7. Day 7 — Luxor, West Bank The Valley of the Kings and Hatshepsut's temple. Karnak later in the day.
  8. Day 8 — To Hurghada Cross the desert to the Red Sea and rest.
  9. Day 9 — The reefs A boat day to the Giftun Islands, or a dive trip.
  10. Day 10 — Departure A last swim, then fly home from Hurghada.

Why this order

Ending at the sea gives the trip a slow finish after early starts at the temples. Flying straight to Aswan saves a long journey south.

Variations

With a Nile cruise

Replace days 4–7 with a cruise between Aswan and Luxor. It stops at Kom Ombo and Edfu, and you unpack only once. Book Abu Simbel as a separate trip from Aswan. Read Nile cruise or hotels?

With Sharm El Sheikh

Fly to Sharm El Sheikh instead of driving to Hurghada, for Ras Mohammed and the Tiran reefs. Read Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh?

With the desert

Swap the Red Sea for a night in the White Desert from Cairo.

Frequently asked questions

Is 10 days enough for Egypt?

For the Nile Valley and a few days by the sea, yes. Alexandria, Siwa and Sinai are best left for another trip.

Should I go to the Red Sea first or last?

Last. It's the best way to recover after the early starts of the temple days.

Can I do Luxor before Aswan?

Yes. Reverse days 4–7, then cross from Luxor to Hurghada, which is the shorter road to the sea.
