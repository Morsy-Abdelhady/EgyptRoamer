What it is

At dawn, balloons rise over the West Bank of Luxor, above the fields, the temples and the cliffs of the Theban hills. They take off from a launch site on the West Bank and fly in the early morning, from sunrise into the first hours after it. Several companies operate flights, under the supervision of the Egyptian Civil Aviation Authority.

The morning

  • Before dawn. Pick-ups are well before sunrise, because the flight is timed to the dawn. The balloons take off from a launch site on the West Bank.
  • Over the fields. From the basket you look over the farmland along the Nile as the sun comes up.
  • The West Bank below. Below you lie the archaeological sites of the West Bank, with the river and the East Bank behind them. After landing you are already on the West Bank.

Before you book

Each morning depends on the weather: on 13 March 2026, for example, the Civil Aviation Authority cancelled the West Bank flights after warnings of strong winds and unsettled weather in the early morning. Book the flight early in your stay in Luxor, so a cancelled morning can still be moved. Check what is included on the partner's page before you book.

Good to know

  • In 2026, commercial flights paused for the summer, from 1 June to 30 August, because of the heat. If you travel in summer, check that flights are running.
  • Listen to the crew's briefing, especially the landing position, and keep to it.
  • A cancellation is a safety decision. Don't look for a company that will fly when others don't.

Who it suits

Choose a flight if you want to see the West Bank from above at dawn and don't mind a very early start. Ballooning carries real risks: there have been fatal accidents, the worst in 2013. Our guide A Hot-Air Balloon over Luxor explains how the flights work and what can stop them.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the balloons fly?

Over Luxor's West Bank: the fields along the Nile and the archaeological sites, with the river and the East Bank in view.

Can a flight be cancelled?

Yes. The Civil Aviation Authority can cancel flights for wind or unstable weather, as it did on 13 March 2026. Keep a spare morning.

Do balloons fly in summer?

In 2026, commercial flights paused from 1 June to 30 August at the operators' request, because of the summer heat. Check before you travel in those months.

Is a balloon flight safe?

Flights are supervised by the Egyptian Civil Aviation Authority, but there have been fatal accidents, the worst in 2013. Follow the crew's instructions and accept weather cancellations.
