The Great Pyramid did not appear from nowhere. South of Giza, at Saqqara and Dahshur, you can follow how the Egyptians learned to build pyramids: from a stepped tomb to the first true pyramid. Both sites belong to the necropolis of ancient Memphis, and they are often much quieter than Giza.

Saqqara

Saqqara stretches along the edge of the desert plateau, with Abusir to the north and Dahshur to the south.

The Step Pyramid of Djoser

Djoser was the second king of the 3rd dynasty. His architect Imhotep designed a new kind of royal tomb: a pyramid in six stages. Around it lies a huge complex of halls and courts, where buildings once made of mud brick, wood and reed were built in fine limestone for the first time.

The Pyramid Texts

Unas, the last king of the 5th dynasty, was the first to have the walls of his burial chambers inscribed with the Pyramid Texts. These spells were meant to protect the king and give him life in the afterlife. The kings of the 6th dynasty continued the practice.

Tombs and the Serapeum

Around the royal pyramids, Old Kingdom nobles were buried in mastabas, tombs whose carved walls show scenes of daily life. From the New Kingdom on, the sacred Apis bulls were buried in large underground galleries, the most famous of which is the Serapeum.

Dahshur

Dahshur lies just south of Saqqara. Its two great pyramids were built by King Sneferu of the 4th dynasty.

  • The Bent Pyramid: an early attempt at a true pyramid. Its first angle, 52°, was too steep, so the upper part was built at 43.5°, which gives it its double slope. It is the only Old Kingdom pyramid with two entrances.
  • The Red Pyramid: Sneferu's second pyramid, built at the lower angle of 43° from the start. It is the first true pyramid to be completed successfully.

Why go

At Dahshur you see the step from the Bent Pyramid to the Red Pyramid, the experiment that made the Great Pyramid possible.

Adding them to your trip

  • Saqqara and Dahshur are usually visited together, often with Giza or the ruins of Memphis.
  • A car and driver, or a guided tour, is the practical way to reach them.
  • Bring sun protection and water: the plateaus are open, with little shade.

Frequently asked questions

Are Saqqara and Dahshur part of a World Heritage site?

Yes. With Giza, Abusir and Abu Rawash, they belong to the Memphis area, added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1979.

Which is older, the Step Pyramid or the Great Pyramid?

The Step Pyramid of Djoser, from the 3rd dynasty. The Great Pyramid was built later, in the 4th dynasty, after Sneferu's pyramids at Dahshur.

Can I go inside?

Some pyramids and tombs open to visitors, and this changes. Check on the day.
