Saint Catherine, in the mountains of South Sinai, is best known for its monastery. The monastery stands inside a much larger protected area, which holds the highest mountains in Egypt and a rare desert wildlife. This guide is based on the facts given by Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency, which manages the country's protected areas. For the monastery and the climb of Mount Sinai, read our Mount Sinai and Saint Catherine guide.

The protected area in brief

  • Declared: 1988.
  • Area: 4,412 km².
  • Type: protected landscape.
  • Where: South Sinai Governorate, about 500 km from Cairo.

Egypt's highest peaks

The area holds the highest mountain peaks in Egypt, among them Gebel Katherina (Mount Catherine), Gebel Musa (Mount Moses), Serbal, Umm Shomar and Al-Thabt. According to the Agency, they rose as a result of the great African rift, about 24 million years ago, which also opened the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aqaba.

The Agency describes the mountain views, and the oases around the springs and wells, as unique in the world.

Wildlife

  • Mammals: among them the Nubian ibex, the Dorcas gazelle, the rock hyrax, the Sinai leopard, the wolf, the hyena, the fox, the hedgehog, the spiny mouse and the jerboa.
  • Reptiles: 27 kinds, including snakes and lizards.
  • Plants: the area has 22 of the 28 plant species found in Sinai that the Agency describes as unique in the world, among them many medicinal and poisonous plants.

Heritage

The protected area holds a large number of churches and monasteries, such as Saint Catherine's Monastery, and remains from the Byzantine period, as well as monuments from Pharaonic and later times.

How to visit

  • The monastery and Mount Sinai: read our Mount Sinai and Saint Catherine guide.
  • Protect the place: never take plants or stones, and leave no rubbish.
  • Entry fees and rules: they can change, so check with the Environmental Affairs Agency or your operator before you go.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Saint Catherine Protected Area?

A protected area of 4,412 km² in the mountains of South Sinai, declared in 1988, which holds Egypt's highest peaks and Saint Catherine's Monastery.

Where are the highest mountains in Egypt?

In the Saint Catherine Protected Area, according to the Agency: among them Mount Catherine, Mount Moses, Serbal, Umm Shomar and Al-Thabt.

What animals live in the Saint Catherine Protected Area?

Among others, the Nubian ibex, the Dorcas gazelle, the rock hyrax, the Sinai leopard, the wolf and the hyena, and 27 kinds of reptiles.

How did the mountains of Saint Catherine form?

According to the Agency, they rose in the great African rift, about 24 million years ago, which also opened the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aqaba.
