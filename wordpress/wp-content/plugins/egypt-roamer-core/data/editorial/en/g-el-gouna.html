On the Red Sea coast, 20 kilometres north of Hurghada, El Gouna is a town built on water, a group of islands separated by lagoons. It is an alternative to staying in Hurghada itself. This guide explains what it is and who it suits.

What El Gouna is

El Gouna was founded in 1990 by the Egyptian businessman Samih Sawiris and is developed by Orascom Development. The town is made up of 20 islands surrounded by lagoons, along about 10 kilometres of coastline, in the Red Sea Governorate. Hurghada International Airport is about 25 kilometres away.

The areas

The town has three main areas:

  • Downtown.
  • Abu Tig Marina, the town's marina.
  • Tamr Henna Square.

On and under the water

El Gouna is known for water sports. The steady winds of this coast have made it a well-known spot for kitesurfing and windsurfing. Beginners can try diving with an instructor: see our Red Sea diving trip, and compare the coast in our guide Snorkelling in Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh.

The film festival

Since 2017 the town has hosted the El Gouna Film Festival. If you travel around the festival, check its dates when you book your stay.

Who El Gouna suits

  • Water sports: kitesurfers and divers who want the sea close at hand.
  • A town of its own: travellers who prefer a lagoon town to a hotel on the long Hurghada coast.
  • A base on the coast: like Hurghada, El Gouna works as a base for a day trip to Luxor. Read our Luxor guide.

Frequently asked questions

How far is El Gouna from Hurghada?

About 20 kilometres north of Hurghada, and about 25 kilometres from Hurghada International Airport.

Who built El Gouna?

It was founded in 1990 by Samih Sawiris and is developed by Orascom Development.

Is El Gouna good for kitesurfing?

Yes. Its steady winds make it one of the coast's best-known kitesurfing spots.
