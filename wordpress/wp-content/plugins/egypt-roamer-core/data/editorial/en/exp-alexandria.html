What it is

A full day from Cairo to Egypt's Mediterranean city and back. Alexandria was founded by Alexander the Great and became the capital of the Ptolemies. A day trip strings together its best-known sites with a seafood lunch on the Corniche, so you see a side of Egypt that is Greek, Roman and Mediterranean as much as pharaonic.

What you see

  • The Citadel of Qaitbay: the 15th-century sea fort built on the site of the ancient Pharos lighthouse, reusing its stone.
  • The Bibliotheca Alexandrina: the modern library on the seafront, a tribute to the ancient Library of Alexandria.
  • Kom el-Shoqafa: Roman-era catacombs cut deep into the rock, where Egyptian gods appear in Greco-Roman style.
  • Pompey's Pillar: a single granite column on the hill of the ancient Serapeum, raised in fact for the emperor Diocletian.
  • The Corniche and Montaza: the seafront promenade and the former royal gardens at its eastern end.

Tours differ in which of these they include, so compare the itineraries.

Making one day count

The round trip from Cairo takes a large share of the day, so most tours start early and focus on four or five sites. A private car with a guide lets you choose them. Lunch is usually fish, chosen from the ice and grilled or fried as you ask.

Before you book

  • Check which sites are visited inside, and which are seen only from outside.
  • Ask whether entry tickets and lunch are included.
  • Confirm the pickup time and place in Cairo or Giza.

Who it suits

Travellers with a few days in Cairo who want a complete change of scene, and anyone interested in the Greek and Roman story of Egypt. If you have more time, two days in Alexandria are better than one.

Frequently asked questions

Is a day trip to Alexandria worth it?

Yes, if you have a spare day in Cairo. You will see the highlights, though staying a night gives a more relaxed visit.

Can I still see the ancient Lighthouse or Library?

Neither survives. The Citadel of Qaitbay stands on the lighthouse's site, and the modern Bibliotheca Alexandrina honours the ancient library.

Can I go by train instead of a tour?

Yes. Trains run between Cairo and Alexandria throughout the day, and you can move around the city by taxi or ride-hailing app.
