What it is

A day by 4×4 from Marsa Alam into Wadi El-Gemal National Park, on Egypt's southern Red Sea coast. Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency declared it a national park in 2003. Wadi El-Gemal is one of the largest and richest valleys of the Eastern Desert, where flourishing plant life supports many creatures, some of them rare and endangered.

The day

  • Into the mountains. The park reaches about 50 km inland into the mountains of the Eastern Desert, with Gebel Hamata among the highest of them.
  • The valley. The valley's flourishing plant life, which many creatures depend on.
  • Wildlife. The park is home to a great variety of plants and animals, and its coast and islands are important for nesting birds and sea turtles.

  • Wadi El-Gemal is a national park. Its rules protect habitats that are rare in Egypt, so follow them.
