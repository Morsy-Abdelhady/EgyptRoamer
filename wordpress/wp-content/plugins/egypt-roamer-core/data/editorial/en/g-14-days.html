Two weeks is enough to see Egypt without rushing: the great sites of Cairo and the Nile Valley, a day on the Mediterranean, and a few days by the sea or in the desert. It builds on our 7-day and 10-day itineraries. To compare the 7, 10 and 14-day plans, see how to plan your Egypt itinerary.

The plan at a glance

  • Days 1–4: Cairo, with a day trip to Alexandria.
  • Days 5–6: Aswan, with Abu Simbel.
  • Days 7–10: the Nile, by cruise from Aswan to Luxor, or by road with hotel stays.
  • Days 11–14: the Red Sea at Hurghada, or the Western Desert.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 — Arrive in Cairo An easy first evening.
  2. Day 2 — Giza The pyramids early, then the Grand Egyptian Museum.
  3. Day 3 — Old Cairo Coptic and Islamic Cairo, with an evening on Al-Muizz Street. See Cairo at night.
  4. Day 4 — Alexandria A day trip to the Mediterranean city. See our Alexandria day trip.
  5. Day 5 — Fly to Aswan Philae in the afternoon and a felucca at sunset.
  6. Day 6 — Abu Simbel A pre-dawn start for Abu Simbel.
  7. Days 7–9 — Down the Nile A cruise from Aswan to Luxor, with stops at Kom Ombo, the temple of the crocodile god Sobek and of Horus the Elder, and Edfu, the temple of Horus on the west bank. Or travel by road and stay in hotels. Read Nile cruise or hotels?
  8. Day 10 — Luxor The Valley of the Kings on the West Bank, and Karnak and Luxor Temple on the East Bank.
  9. Day 11 — To the Red Sea Cross from Luxor to Hurghada by road.
  10. Days 12–13 — The reefs Snorkelling at the Giftun Islands, or a dive trip.
  11. Day 14 — Departure Fly home from Hurghada.

Pacing

Two weeks gives you room for rest days. Use them: a slow morning in Aswan or an afternoon by the pool after the temple days makes the whole trip better.

Variations

The desert instead of the sea

Replace days 11–14 with a night in the White Desert from Cairo, or several days in Siwa and the Great Sand Sea.

Sharm El Sheikh instead of Hurghada

Fly to Sharm El Sheikh for Ras Mohammed and the Tiran reefs. Read Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh?

Frequently asked questions

Is two weeks too long for Egypt?

No. It lets you see the main sites at a comfortable pace and add the sea or the desert.

Should the Nile cruise go north or south?

Both work. This plan goes north, from Aswan to Luxor, so you can cross from Luxor to the Red Sea.

Can I add Siwa and the Red Sea?

In two weeks it's too much. Choose one, or plan a second trip.
