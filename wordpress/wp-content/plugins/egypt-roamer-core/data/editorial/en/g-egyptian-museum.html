The Egyptian Museum stands on Tahrir Square in downtown Cairo. Egypt's Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities calls it the oldest archaeological museum in the Middle East, with a collection that runs from the Predynastic Period to the Graeco-Roman era. This guide explains what you will see and the museum's rules, from the Ministry's page.

The building

The architect was chosen in 1895 through an international competition, the first of its kind. It was won by the French architect Marcel Dourgnon. Khedive Abbas Helmy II opened the museum in 1902, and it has become a landmark of downtown Cairo.

Highlights

Among the objects the Ministry names:

  • The Narmer Palette, which commemorates the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt under one king.
  • The statues of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, the kings who built the pyramids on the Giza plateau.
  • The complete burials of Yuya and Thuya.
  • Psusennes I and the treasures of Tanis.
  • Papyri, coffins and jewellery, among many other objects.

Two famous groups are no longer here: the royal mummies moved to the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization in 2021, and the objects from Tutankhamun's tomb went to the Grand Egyptian Museum. Our guide to Cairo's museums explains which museum holds what now.

Rules for visitors

The Ministry's page lists the museum's rules. The main ones:

  • Photos: photography with a mobile phone is free. No flash, and don't photograph other visitors or staff. A camera stand needs its own ticket.
  • Bags: large bags, backpacks and parcels bigger than 40 × 40 cm, umbrellas and sharp objects go in the cloakroom. Bags may be searched.
  • Food and drink: not allowed in the galleries, except small bottles of water. No smoking anywhere in the museum.
  • Exhibits: don't touch exhibits or showcases, and don't use flashlights or laser pointers.
  • Children must stay with an adult at all times. No pets.
  • Dress: appropriate clothing is requested.

Services

The Ministry lists a café, a gift shop, an information desk, a lift, a library, a ramped entrance, labels in Braille, toilets, parking and sign language. An audio tour is also offered.

How to visit

  • Where: Tahrir Square, in downtown Cairo.
  • Last tickets: the ticket window closes an hour before the museum.
  • Tickets and opening hours: these change, and Ramadan has its own hours, so check the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities' website before you go.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tutankhamun still in the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir?

No. The objects from Tutankhamun's tomb went to the Grand Egyptian Museum, and the royal mummies moved to the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization in 2021.

What is there to see in the Egyptian Museum?

The Narmer Palette, the statues of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, the burials of Yuya and Thuya, the treasures of Tanis, and papyri, coffins and jewellery from the Predynastic Period to the Graeco-Roman era.

Can I take photos in the Egyptian Museum?

Yes: photography with a mobile phone is free. Flash is not allowed, and a camera stand needs its own ticket.

When did the Egyptian Museum open?

In 1902, opened by Khedive Abbas Helmy II. The building was designed by the French architect Marcel Dourgnon.
