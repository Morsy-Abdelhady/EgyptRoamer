The Hurghada Museum is the first antiquities museum in Egypt's Red Sea Governorate. It is a good break from the beach for anyone staying in Hurghada. This guide uses the facts on the page of Egypt's Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities.

A new kind of museum

The Ministry calls it the first museum of its kind: it was created by the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, the Red Sea Governorate and the private sector working together. The private sector paid for the building.

The collection

The museum holds about 2,000 objects. Its theme is the idea of beauty in Egypt, and its objects tell the story of Egypt across its eras:

  • ancient Egypt;
  • the Graeco-Roman period;
  • Jewish, Christian and Islamic Egypt;
  • the modern era.

Rules for visitors

The Ministry's page lists the museum's rules. The main ones:

  • Photos: no flash, and don't photograph other visitors or staff. Tripods and monopods are only for commercial filming.
  • Bags: large bags, backpacks and parcels bigger than 40 × 40 cm, umbrellas and sharp objects go in the cloakroom. Bags may be searched.
  • Food and drink: not allowed in the galleries, except small bottles of water. No smoking anywhere in the museum.
  • Exhibits: don't touch exhibits or showcases, and don't use flashlights or laser pointers.
  • Children must stay with an adult at all times. No pets.

The Ministry lists a lift and a gift shop. An audio tour is also offered.

How to visit

  • Morning or evening: the Ministry lists a morning opening and a separate evening opening, so you can visit after a day at sea.
  • Last tickets: the ticket window closes an hour before each opening ends.
  • Tickets and opening hours: these change, and Ramadan has its own hours, so check the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities' website before you go.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the Hurghada Museum?

About 2,000 objects on the theme of beauty in Egypt, from ancient Egypt and the Graeco-Roman period to Jewish, Christian and Islamic Egypt and the modern era.

Is the Hurghada Museum open in the evening?

Yes. The Ministry lists an evening opening as well as a morning one; check the current hours on its website.

Who built the Hurghada Museum?

The Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, the Red Sea Governorate and the private sector together; the private sector paid for the building.

Is the Hurghada Museum the first museum on the Red Sea?

It is the first antiquities museum in the Red Sea Governorate.
