Siwa Oasis is known for its springs, salt lakes and the oracle that Alexander the Great consulted. Around it lies a national park that protects the oasis's desert wildlife as well as its monuments. This guide is based on the facts given by Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency, which manages the country's protected areas. For the oasis itself, read our Siwa guide.

The protected area in brief

  • Declared: 2002.
  • Area: 7,731 km².
  • Type: national park.
  • Where: Matrouh Governorate, about 800 km from Cairo.

Monuments

The Agency describes Siwa as rich in reasons to visit: antiquities, health tourism, safari and desert travel. Among the archaeological sites it names:

  • The Temple of Amun, with inscriptions showing kings making offerings to the gods, and the hall where Alexander the Great was crowned.
  • Gebel al-Mawta, the Mountain of the Dead, with ancient mummies and tombs from Roman times.
  • Dhebiya, with rock-cut tombs from the Graeco-Roman period.
  • Khamisa, with a group of tombs from the Greek period.

Wildlife

  • Plants: more than 40 kinds of wild plants, among them medicinal plants, grazing plants and plants that hold the sand in place, as well as stands of acacia and tamarisk.
  • Mammals: about 28 kinds of wild mammals, among them rare and threatened ones: the striped hyena, the Dorcas and slender-horned gazelles, the red fox and the fennec fox.
  • Reptiles and birds: 32 kinds of reptiles and about 164 kinds of birds, as well as many invertebrates and insects.

Frequently asked questions

Is Siwa a protected area?

Yes. The Siwa Protected Area was declared in 2002, as a national park of 7,731 km².

What monuments are in the Siwa Protected Area?

Among others, the Temple of Amun, the Mountain of the Dead with its Roman tombs, and the Graeco-Roman tombs of Dhebiya and Khamisa.

What animals live around Siwa?

About 28 kinds of wild mammals, among them the striped hyena, the Dorcas and slender-horned gazelles, the red fox and the fennec fox, plus 32 kinds of reptiles and about 164 kinds of birds.

How far is Siwa from Cairo?

About 800 km, according to the Environmental Affairs Agency.
