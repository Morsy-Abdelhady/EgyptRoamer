Egypt's Western Desert offers two classic trips from Cairo: the oasis of Siwa near the Libyan border, and a night among the chalk formations of the White Desert beyond Bahariya. Both are unforgettable, and both are very different. This guide helps you choose.

At a glance

  • Siwa is a living oasis: palm groves, salt lakes and springs, a mud-brick old town and an Amazigh community with its own language. The Great Sand Sea begins at its edge.
  • The White Desert is pure landscape: wind-carved chalk, the volcanic hills of the Black Desert, and a night camping under the stars.

Siwa: the oasis

Siwa lies in the Western Desert near the Libyan border. It has about 200 springs and is known for its dates and olives. Its old walled settlements of Siwa and Aghurmi stand on rock outcrops, and at Aghurmi are the ruins of the temple of the Oracle of Amun, consulted by Alexander the Great. From the oasis, 4×4 trips go out into the dunes of the Great Sand Sea. Read more in our Siwa guide.

The White Desert: the landscape

Trips leave Cairo for Bahariya Oasis, then cross the Black Desert and Crystal Mountain to the White Desert, a protected area where the wind has shaped chalk into giant mushrooms and pillars. The night is spent at a simple camp among the formations. Read A night in the White Desert.

How they compare

  • Time. The White Desert works as a two-day, one-night trip from Cairo. Siwa needs more: the oasis deserves several nights, plus a long travel day each way.
  • Comfort. In Siwa you sleep in guesthouses or hotels. White Desert camps are basic, usually without toilets or showers.
  • Culture. Siwa has a traditional community and a long history. The White Desert is about nature and silence.
  • Season. Both are best from October to April. Desert nights are cold in winter.

Which should you choose?

  • Short on time: the White Desert.
  • Want culture as well as landscape: Siwa.
  • Love the desert: both, on separate trips.

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit both on one trip?

They lie in different parts of the Western Desert. Combining them takes a long desert circuit, so most people choose one per trip.

Which is better for families?

Siwa, with real beds and more to do in the oasis. The White Desert suits children who are happy with basic camping.

Do I need a tour?

For the desert itself, yes: trips need a 4×4 and a licensed local driver. In Siwa town you can explore on your own.
