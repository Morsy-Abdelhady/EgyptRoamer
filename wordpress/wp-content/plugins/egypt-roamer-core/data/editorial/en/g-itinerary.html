A good Egypt itinerary starts with one question: how many days do you have? With 7 days, stay in the Nile Valley: Cairo, Luxor and Aswan. With 10 days, add a few days on the Red Sea. With 14 days, add Alexandria too, take your time on the Nile, and finish by the sea or in the desert. This page helps you choose, then sends you to the full day-by-day plan.

Choose your length

  • 7 days in Egypt: Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, with Abu Simbel. Enough for the highlights of the Nile Valley, with early starts.
  • 10 days in Egypt: the same Nile Valley sites, then three days on the Red Sea at Hurghada to rest, snorkel or dive.
  • 14 days in Egypt: Cairo with a day trip to Alexandria, Aswan and Abu Simbel, the Nile from Aswan to Luxor by cruise or by road, and the Red Sea or the Western Desert to finish.

The core of every itinerary

All three plans are built on the same three stops:

  • Cairo: the pyramids and the Sphinx at Giza, the Grand Egyptian Museum, and Coptic and Islamic Cairo.
  • Luxor: the temples of Karnak and Luxor on the East Bank, and the Valley of the Kings and Hatshepsut's temple on the West Bank.
  • Aswan: the temple of Philae, a felucca on the Nile at sunset, and a pre-dawn trip to Abu Simbel.

Between Cairo and the south, fly or take the overnight sleeper train. Between Luxor and Aswan, travel by road, train or boat. By road you can stop at the temples of Edfu and Kom Ombo.

Where the time goes

Transfers take longer than they look on a map. Leave a buffer on your departure day, and avoid a very early international flight on the day you return from the south.

A Nile cruise or hotels?

A cruise between Aswan and Luxor stops at Kom Ombo and Edfu, and you unpack only once. Hotels give you more time in Luxor and Aswan themselves. In every plan, a cruise can replace the Luxor and Aswan days. Read Nile cruise or hotels? and see our Nile cruise from Aswan to Luxor.

Adding the sea or the desert

  • The Red Sea: end the trip at Hurghada, reached from Luxor by road, for reefs, islands and rest. Or fly to Sharm El Sheikh for Ras Mohammed and the Tiran reefs. Read Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh?
  • The desert: a night in the White Desert from Cairo, or several days in Siwa and the Great Sand Sea.

Ending at the sea gives the trip a slow finish after the early starts of the temple days. In two weeks, choose the sea or Siwa, not both.

What to leave for next time

In one week, Alexandria, Siwa and the Western Desert, Sinai and a longer Red Sea stay each deserve their own trip. Trying to squeeze them in means you see none of them properly. Most people who return to Egypt say they wish they had slowed down the first time.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Egypt?

Seven days covers the highlights of the Nile Valley with early starts. Ten days lets you add the Red Sea. Fourteen days lets you add Alexandria and the sea or the desert at a comfortable pace.

What is the best Egypt itinerary for a first trip?

Cairo, Luxor and Aswan. They hold most of the monuments people come to see, and they are connected by short flights, trains and the river.

Is a Nile cruise better than hotels?

A cruise is relaxing and includes Edfu and Kom Ombo. Hotels give you more time in Luxor and Aswan themselves. It depends on your pace.

Should I go to the Red Sea first or last?

Last. It is the best way to recover after the early starts of the temple days.
