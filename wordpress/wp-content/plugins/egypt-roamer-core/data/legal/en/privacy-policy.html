What we handle, and why

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message, plus the site language you wrote from. We use them only to reply to you. Messages are stored in our website's administration area and forwarded to our inbox.
  • Newsletter sign-up. Your email address, the site language, any interests you choose, where on the site you signed up, and the date. We use them only for the newsletter you asked for. We have not sent any newsletter yet; before the first one, we will ask you to confirm your address.
  • Clicks on partner links. When you use a partner link (see our Affiliate Disclosure), we record the time, the offer, the page and part of the page it was on, the call to action and the site language. We do not record your IP address, your browser or a cookie for this. We use these records to see which recommendations are useful.
  • Page-view statistics. When a page loads, your browser tells our website which page it is (the address without anything after a “?”), the site language, the type of page, the website you came from (its domain only, such as google.com, never the page) and whether your screen is phone, tablet or computer size. We add one to a daily total for that combination. We do not set a cookie, give you an identifier or store your IP address or browser details for this, so we cannot tell visitors apart. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signal, nothing is sent. We use the totals to see which pages are useful and how people find us.
  • Google Analytics, only with your consent. If you accept statistics cookies in our cookie banner, your browser loads Google Analytics. It records the pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location, your device and browser, and some actions on the page, such as scrolling and clicks on links to other sites, using the cookies listed in our Cookie Policy. We use the reports to see which pages are useful and how people find us. If you do not accept, Google Analytics is not loaded. Google signals and the sharing of this data with Google for other purposes are turned off.
  • Spam protection. To limit abuse of our forms, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address with the date for 10 minutes. It is not stored with your message.
  • Your browser's own storage. The site remembers your language and the items you save with the heart icon in your browser's local storage, and whether you have already seen the homepage introduction in session storage. This stays on your device; we do not receive it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising trackers on this site. See our Cookie Policy for the cookies that are set, and when.

Services that process data for us

  • GoDaddy hosts the website and its database.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it against attacks and automated abuse; it sets the security cookie described in the Cookie Policy.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, which runs only if you accept statistics cookies (see above).
  • Unsplash serves some of the photos on this site, so your browser connects to Unsplash's servers when those photos load.
  • Our partners (currently Viator, GetYourGuide and Klook for tours and activities, and Trip.com for hotels): when you follow a partner link, you leave our site. What you do there, including any booking, is handled by the partner under its own privacy policy.

When your browser requests a page, GoDaddy's and Cloudflare's servers receive your IP address and browser details, as with any website; they use them to deliver and protect the site and may keep them in technical logs. These services may process data outside the United Kingdom.

How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: as long as we need them to answer you and handle any follow-up, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.
  • Partner-link click records: 730 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Page-view totals: 25 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Google Analytics: event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months, then deleted by Google.
  • Spam-protection data: 10 minutes.

What you can ask us

Email info@egyptroamer.com to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, or to unsubscribe from the newsletter. We will answer your request. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. You can change or withdraw your consent to statistics cookies at any time in the cookie banner settings, which you can reopen on any page.
