Egypt Roamer does not use advertising or social-media tracking cookies. Statistics cookies from Google Analytics are set only if you accept them in our cookie banner. This page lists everything this website stores in your browser.

Cookies

Other storage in your browser

These are not cookies and are never sent to us. They make the site work the way you left it:

  • Local storage: your chosen language, and the places and experiences you save with the heart icon. They stay until you remove them or clear your browser data.
  • Session storage: whether you have already seen the homepage introduction during this visit. It is deleted when you close the browser tab.

Partner websites

When you follow a partner link (for example to Viator), you leave Egypt Roamer. The partner's website sets its own cookies, including the ones that let the partner know you came from us. Those are covered by the partner's own cookie policy. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Your control

You can delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. You can change or withdraw your consent to statistics cookies at any time in the cookie banner settings, which you can reopen on any page.
