Egypt Roamer is an independent travel publication about Egypt. Some links on this site are affiliate links: when you book through one of them, the booking partner may pay us a commission. You pay the same price as you would booking with the partner directly.

Our partners

We currently take part in the Viator affiliate programme. We update this page when our partners change.

How affiliate links work here

  • Partner offers appear next to our articles, labelled "Book with our partners", with a short notice about commission.
  • All affiliate links pass through an address on our own site that starts with /go/, then take you to the partner's website. We count the click (which offer, on which page, in which language) but not who you are: we do not record your IP address or set a cookie for it.
  • Affiliate links are marked for search engines with rel="sponsored".

We do not sell travel

We do not take bookings or payments, and we are not a travel agency or tour operator. When you book, you book with the partner, under the partner's terms. Availability, prices, payment, changes, cancellations and refunds are handled by the partner. For questions about a booking, contact the partner you booked with.

A partner's appearance on Egypt Roamer does not mean that the partner endorses us, and we do not guarantee any partner's prices or availability.

Our independence

Commission never decides what we write. A partner cannot pay for a place in our articles, and our editorial text is written before any offer is placed next to it.
