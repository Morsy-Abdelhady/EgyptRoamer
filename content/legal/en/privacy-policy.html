What we handle, and why

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message, plus the site language you wrote from. We use them only to reply to you. Messages are stored in our website's administration area and forwarded to our inbox.
  • Newsletter sign-up. Your email address, the site language, any interests you choose, where on the site you signed up, and the date. We use them only for the newsletter you asked for. We have not sent any newsletter yet; before the first one, we will ask you to confirm your address.
  • Clicks on partner links. When you use a partner link (see our Affiliate Disclosure), we record the time, the offer, the page and part of the page it was on, the call to action and the site language. We do not record your IP address, your browser or a cookie for this. We use these records to see which recommendations are useful.
  • Spam protection. To limit abuse of our forms, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address with the date for 10 minutes. It is not stored with your message.
  • Your browser's own storage. The site remembers your language and the items you save with the heart icon in your browser's local storage, and whether you have already seen the homepage introduction in session storage. This stays on your device; we do not receive it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site. See our Cookie Policy for the only cookie that is set.

Services that process data for us

  • GoDaddy hosts the website and its database.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it against attacks and automated abuse; it sets the security cookie described in the Cookie Policy.
  • Unsplash serves some of the photos on this site, so your browser connects to Unsplash's servers when those photos load.
  • Our partners (currently Viator): when you follow a partner link, you leave our site. What you do there, including any booking, is handled by the partner under its own privacy policy.

These services may process data outside Egypt.

How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: as long as we need them to answer you and handle any follow-up, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you.
  • Partner-link click records: 730 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Spam-protection data: 10 minutes.

What you can ask us

Email info@egyptroamer.com to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, or to unsubscribe from the newsletter. We will answer your request. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

This website is written for adult travellers and is not aimed at children.

Changes to this page
