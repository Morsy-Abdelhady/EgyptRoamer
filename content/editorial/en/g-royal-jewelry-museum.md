The Royal Jewelry Museum in Alexandria shows the treasures of Egypt's royal family, the dynasty founded by Muhammad Ali Pasha in 1805, which ruled for 150 years, until 1952. This guide uses the facts on the page of Egypt's Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities.

## The palace {#palace}

The museum is a palace built in 1919 by Zaynab Fahmi in Zizinya, Alexandria. It was the home of Fatma al-Zahra', daughter of Prince Ali Haidar, a descendant of Muhammad Ali Pasha.

The palace mixes European and Islamic styles. Paintings, gilded ceilings and mosaics decorate its rooms. It has two wings, east and west, joined by a corridor, and each wing has a basement and two floors.

## The collection {#collection}

The halls show what the family gathered over its years in power. Among the pieces the Ministry names:

- **A collar set with diamonds and emeralds** that belonged to the dynasty's founder, Muhammad Ali Pasha.
- **A gold chessboard.**
- **Gold binoculars set with diamonds, rubies and emeralds.**
- **Gold cups set with precious stones.**
- **Jewellery sets made by Europe's greatest designers,** once worn by the queens and princesses of Egypt's royal family.

## Rules for visitors {#rules}

The Ministry's page lists the museum's rules. The main ones:

- **Photos:** no flash, and don't photograph other visitors or staff. Tripods and monopods are only for permitted commercial filming.
- **Bags:** large bags, backpacks and parcels bigger than 40 × 40 cm, umbrellas and sharp objects go in the cloakroom. Bags may be searched.
- **Food and drink:** not allowed in the galleries, except small bottles of water. No smoking anywhere in the museum.
- **Exhibits:** don't touch exhibits or showcases, and don't use flashlights or laser pointers.
- **Children** must stay with an adult at all times. No pets.

The Ministry lists a gift shop, a café and parking.

## How to visit {#visiting}

- **Where:** Zizinya, Alexandria.
- **Last tickets:** the ticket window closes an hour before the museum.
- **Tickets and opening hours:** these change, and Ramadan has its own hours, so check the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities' website before you go.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? What is in the Royal Jewelry Museum?
The treasures of the Muhammad Ali dynasty, which ruled Egypt from 1805 to 1952: a diamond and emerald collar of Muhammad Ali Pasha, a gold chessboard, jewelled gold binoculars and cups, and jewellery sets made in Europe for Egypt's queens and princesses.

?? Whose palace is the Royal Jewelry Museum?
It was built in 1919 by Zaynab Fahmi and was the home of Fatma al-Zahra', daughter of Prince Ali Haidar, a descendant of Muhammad Ali Pasha.

?? Where is the Royal Jewelry Museum?
In Zizinya, Alexandria.

?? Is flash photography allowed in the Royal Jewelry Museum?
No. Flash is not allowed, and tripods and monopods are only for permitted commercial filming.
