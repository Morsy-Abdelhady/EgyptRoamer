## What it is {#what-it-is}

An evening on the Nile in Cairo aboard a restaurant boat. You get a dinner buffet or set menu, live music and usually a show, while the boat cruises past the lights of the city. It's one of the most popular evening activities in Cairo, and an easy way to see the river after dark.

## What the evening is like {#what-its-like}

Boats leave from docks along the Corniche and cruise the central stretch of the river. Most evenings include:

- **Dinner**, often a buffet of Egyptian and international dishes.
- **Live music**, usually Egyptian and Arabic popular songs.
- **A tanoura performance**: the spinning dance with multi-coloured skirts, related to Sufi whirling.
- On many boats, **a belly dance show**.

The mood is festive rather than intimate. Expect other groups, families and celebrations on board.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Travellers who want an easy, entertaining evening with a view, families, and groups. If you prefer quiet, a **felucca** ride at sunset, just the sail, the water and the city, may suit you better. Many visitors do both.

## Choosing a boat {#choosing}

> **What to check before you book**
> - **Food and drink:** buffet or set menu, and whether drinks are included (alcohol is not served on every boat).
> - **The show programme:** tanoura, belly dance, or both.
> - **Where the boat departs**, and whether transfers from your hotel are included.
> - **Seating:** tables by the windows or on an open deck.

## Combine it with {#combine}

A day in Islamic Cairo or Coptic Cairo, ending with dinner on the river. Our [Cairo guide](/destinations/cairo/) covers the areas and a three-day plan.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is a Nile dinner cruise worth it?
For a lively evening with dinner, music and the city lights, yes. For a quieter view of the river, choose a felucca at sunset instead.

?? What is the tanoura dance?
A traditional Egyptian dance in which performers spin continuously while turning layered, brightly coloured skirts. It's related to the whirling of Sufi practice.

?? What should I wear?
Smart-casual is fine. Bring a light layer, since it can be cool on the open deck in winter.
