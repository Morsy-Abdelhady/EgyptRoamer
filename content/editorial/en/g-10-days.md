Ten days lets you add something the [seven-day itinerary](/guides/7-days-in-egypt-itinerary/) has to leave out: the sea. This plan covers the great sites of the Nile Valley, then ends with three days on the Red Sea to rest, snorkel or dive.

## The plan at a glance {#at-a-glance}

- **Days 1–3: Cairo.** The pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum, Islamic and Coptic Cairo.
- **Days 4–5: Aswan.** Philae, the Nile by felucca, and Abu Simbel.
- **Days 6–7: Luxor.** The East Bank temples and the West Bank tombs.
- **Days 8–10: Hurghada.** Reefs, islands and rest.

Fly from Cairo to Aswan, travel north to Luxor by road, train or boat, cross to Hurghada by road, and fly home from Hurghada.

## Day by day {#day-by-day}

+ **Day 1 — Arrive in Cairo** Settle in and have an easy first evening.
+ **Day 2 — Giza** [The pyramids and the Sphinx](/experiences/pyramids-of-giza-sphinx-private-tour/) early, then the Grand Egyptian Museum.
+ **Day 3 — Old Cairo** Coptic Cairo and Islamic Cairo, with an evening on Al-Muizz Street. See [Cairo at night](/guides/cairo-at-night/).
+ **Day 4 — Aswan** Fly south. Philae in the afternoon and a felucca at sunset.
+ **Day 5 — Abu Simbel** A pre-dawn start for [Abu Simbel](/experiences/abu-simbel-day-trip-from-aswan/), back in Aswan by the afternoon.
+ **Day 6 — To Luxor** North by road, stopping at Kom Ombo and Edfu. Luxor Temple at dusk.
+ **Day 7 — Luxor, West Bank** [The Valley of the Kings and Hatshepsut's temple](/experiences/valley-of-the-kings-hatshepsut-temple/). Karnak later in the day.
+ **Day 8 — To Hurghada** Cross the desert to the Red Sea and rest.
+ **Day 9 — The reefs** A boat day to the Giftun Islands, or a [dive trip](/experiences/red-sea-diving-two-reef-dives/).
+ **Day 10 — Departure** A last swim, then fly home from Hurghada.

> **Why this order**
> Ending at the sea gives the trip a slow finish after early starts at the temples. Flying straight to Aswan saves a long journey south.

## Variations {#variations}

### With a Nile cruise
Replace days 4–7 with a cruise between Aswan and Luxor. It stops at Kom Ombo and Edfu, and you unpack only once. Book Abu Simbel as a separate trip from Aswan. Read [Nile cruise or hotels?](/guides/nile-cruise-or-hotels-luxor-aswan/)

### With Sharm El Sheikh
Fly to Sharm El Sheikh instead of driving to Hurghada, for Ras Mohammed and the Tiran reefs. Read [Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh?](/guides/snorkelling-hurghada-or-sharm/)

### With the desert
Swap the Red Sea for a night in the [White Desert](/guides/white-desert-night/) from Cairo.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is 10 days enough for Egypt?
For the Nile Valley and a few days by the sea, yes. Alexandria, Siwa and Sinai are best left for another trip.

?? Should I go to the Red Sea first or last?
Last. It's the best way to recover after the early starts of the temple days.

?? Can I do Luxor before Aswan?
Yes. Reverse days 4–7, then cross from Luxor to Hurghada, which is the shorter road to the sea.
