The Citadel of Sultan Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi, known in English as Saladin, stands on the Muqattam Hills above Cairo. Egypt's Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities calls it one of the most iconic monuments of Islamic Cairo, and one of the most impressive fortresses of the Middle Ages. From its walls you still get a wide view over the city. This guide uses the facts on the Ministry's page.

## History {#history}

Saladin was the first to order a fortress on the Muqattam Hills, in 572 AH (1176 AD), but it was not finished in his lifetime. It was completed under Sultan al-Kamil ibn al-Adil (604 AH, 1207 AD), who chose to live there. From then on, the Citadel was the official residence of Egypt's rulers.

In the mid-nineteenth century, Khedive Ismail moved the official residence to Abdeen Palace in downtown Cairo. The Citadel had seen some of the most important events in Egyptian history, from its building to the end of the Muhammad Ali Dynasty, which ruled from the early nineteenth century until the 1952 revolution.

The Citadel is part of Historic Cairo, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

## What to see inside {#inside}

Over the centuries, rulers added buildings to the Citadel. The Ministry names these:

- **The Mosque of Muhammad Ali Pasha,** which dominates the Citadel.
- **The mosque of the Mamluk sultan al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun.**
- **The Mosque of Sulayman Pasha al-Khadim,** from the Ottoman era.
- **Museums:** the Police Museum, the Al-Jawhara Palace Museum and the Military Museum.

## How to visit {#visiting}

- **Where:** on the Muqattam Hills, above Islamic Cairo. Our guide to [Islamic Cairo](/guides/islamic-cairo-guide/) pairs it with the Mosque of Sultan Hassan.
- **Tickets:** one ticket covers the whole Citadel area. Cars, minibuses and buses need their own vehicle ticket.
- **Tickets and opening hours:** these change, so check the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities' website before you go.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Who built the Cairo Citadel?
Saladin (Salah al-Din al-Ayyubi) ordered it in 1176. It was completed under Sultan al-Kamil in 1207, who made it the residence of Egypt's rulers.

?? What is inside the Cairo Citadel?
The Mosque of Muhammad Ali Pasha, the mosque of al-Nasir Muhammad ibn Qalawun, the Ottoman-era Mosque of Sulayman Pasha al-Khadim, and the Police Museum, the Al-Jawhara Palace Museum and the Military Museum.

?? Is the Mosque of Muhammad Ali inside the Citadel?
Yes. It dominates the Citadel, and one area ticket covers it.

?? How long did the Citadel serve as the seat of government?
From the early thirteenth century, under al-Kamil, until Khedive Ismail moved the residence to Abdeen Palace in the mid-nineteenth century.
