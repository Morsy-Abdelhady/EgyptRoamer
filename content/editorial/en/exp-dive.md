## What it is {#what-it-is}

A day on a dive boat with two guided reef dives in the Red Sea, from Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh. The Red Sea is known for clear water, colourful coral and abundant reef fish, often just a short boat ride from the coast.

## What a dive day looks like {#dive-day}

- Check-in at the dive centre or on the boat: paperwork, equipment fitting and a briefing.
- The boat sails to the first site. The guide explains the dive plan, the depth and what you might see.
- **First dive**, then a surface interval on board with lunch or snacks.
- **Second dive** at a different reef, then back to port in the afternoon.

Snorkellers can often join the same boat.

## Beginners and certified divers {#requirements}

- **No certification:** you can do a *try-dive* (introductory dive) with an instructor at shallow depth after a briefing. It's a good way to find out whether diving is for you.
- **Certified divers:** guided dives at depths within your certification level. Bring your certification card and logbook.
- **Health:** you'll be asked to complete a medical questionnaire. Some conditions require a doctor's sign-off. Don't fly within the recommended interval after diving.

> **Choosing a dive centre**
> - Look for recognised training-agency affiliation and well-maintained equipment.
> - Ask about group sizes per guide.
> - Choose centres that use fixed moorings rather than anchoring on reefs, and that give a proper briefing.
> - Tell them your real experience level: currents vary between sites.

## Where to dive {#where}

- **From [Hurghada](/destinations/hurghada/):** the Giftun Islands and nearby reefs.
- **From [Sharm El Sheikh](/destinations/sharm/):** Ras Mohammed National Park and the Straits of Tiran; the *Thistlegorm* wreck for experienced divers.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Can I dive without any experience?
Yes, as a try-dive with an instructor, at shallow depth, after a briefing. You'll need to be in reasonable health.

?? When is the best time to dive the Red Sea?
It's possible all year. Water is warmest from late summer into autumn and coolest in late winter, when a thicker wetsuit helps.

?? What about sea life encounters?
Reef fish, turtles and rays are common on many sites. Encounters with larger animals depend on the site and the season, and nothing is guaranteed.
