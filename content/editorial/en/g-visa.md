Most visitors need a visa to enter Egypt. This guide explains the two official ways to get a tourist visa, using only what the Egyptian government's own portals and embassy pages say. Rules and fees can change, so check the official portal before you travel. Last checked: 27 October 2026.

## Who needs a visa {#who-needs}

According to the official Egypt e-Visa Portal, run by Egypt's Ministry of Interior, a citizen of a foreign country who wishes to enter Egypt generally must first obtain an e-Visa.

The portal lists the nationalities that can apply for an e-Visa online. They include the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, all European Union countries, Russia, China, Japan, South Korea, India, Brazil and Mexico, among others. Check the full list on the portal for your passport. If your country is not on it, contact the Egyptian embassy or consulate in your country.

## The e-Visa {#e-visa}

The e-Visa is an official document permitting entry into Egypt. You apply and pay online, and a link to download it is sent to you by email. Passport officers at the port of arrival can check its validity on their system.

The tourist e-Visa comes in two types. The fees listed on the official portal are:

- **Single entry (tourism):** US$30
- **Multiple entry (tourism):** US$65

You pay on the portal with a Visa card, MasterCard or other debit card. The portal says you must create your e-Visa application **at least 7 days before your departure**.

As with any visa, the portal notes that Egyptian port authorities can refuse entry to an e-Visa holder without explanation.

## How to apply, step by step {#how-to-apply}

The official portal lists these steps:

1. Create an account on the Egypt e-Visa Portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) and confirm your registration.
2. Sign in and select Apply Now.
3. Choose the type of visa and read the instructions carefully.
4. Fill in the application form.
5. Pay by Visa card, MasterCard or other debit card.
6. Wait for the approval email, then download and print the e-Visa from your account.
7. Show your printed e-Visa to the immigration officer when you arrive.

Apply only on the official portal. Other websites that offer to arrange an Egyptian visa are not the government portal.

## Visa on arrival {#on-arrival}

Egypt also has an official Visa on Arrival platform (apps.visaonarrival.gov.eg). You use the website, a self-service machine or the mobile app, pay securely, enter your details and receive a QR code by email or as a download, which you show to the officer on arrival. The platform says it can be printed if you prefer.

According to the Embassy of Egypt in Washington, from 1 September 2026 all Egyptian airports have moved from the old visa-on-arrival stickers to this electronic QR code.

Not every nationality can get a visa on arrival. The embassy page says that holders of passports from countries on the pre-approval list cannot get a visa on arrival, and advises other applicants to get the visa in advance. If you are not sure about your passport, get the e-Visa before you fly or ask the Egyptian embassy in your country.

## What to bring {#what-to-bring}

The official e-Visa Portal lists these entry requirements on arrival:

- A passport valid for **at least 6 months** from your arrival date.
- A printout of your e-Visa.
- Your travel itinerary.
- Hotel bookings, or details of the places you will visit if you travel as a tourist.
- For business or family visits, a supporting letter, for example from your company or an invitation letter.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Do I need a visa to visit Egypt?
Most foreign visitors do. The official e-Visa Portal says a foreign citizen generally must obtain an e-Visa before entering, and it lists the nationalities that can apply online.

?? How much is the Egypt e-Visa?
The official portal lists US$30 for a single entry tourist visa and US$65 for a multiple entry tourist visa.

?? How early should I apply for the Egypt e-Visa?
The portal says you must create your application at least 7 days before your departure.

?? Can I get a visa on arrival in Egypt?
Some nationalities can, through the official Visa on Arrival platform, which gives you a QR code to show at the airport. Holders of passports from countries on the pre-approval list cannot. If in doubt, get the e-Visa in advance.

?? How long must my passport be valid for Egypt?
The official portal says your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date.
