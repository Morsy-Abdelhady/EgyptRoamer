## What it is {#what-it-is}

A full day from your hotel on the Red Sea to Luxor, the site of ancient Thebes. In one day you see the great temple of Karnak on the East Bank, then cross the Nile to the West Bank for the **Valley of the Kings**, the terraced **temple of Hatshepsut** at Deir el-Bahari and the **Colossi of Memnon**. They are all part of the UNESCO site *Ancient Thebes with its Necropolis*.

## The day {#the-day}

- **The road.** The day starts with pickup at your hotel. The drive to Luxor takes about 4.5 hours each way, according to the GetYourGuide option's itinerary.
- **Karnak.** The ancient Egyptians called it *Ipet-Sut*, "the Most Select of Places". Its Great Temple of Amun has a hypostyle hall of 134 columns.
- **The West Bank.** Across the Nile lies the West Bank, the burial ground of Thebes.
- **Valley of the Kings.** Long corridors lead down to royal burial chambers covered in texts and images of the gods. A standard ticket covers a set number of the tombs open that day; Tutankhamun's tomb needs a separate ticket.
- **Hatshepsut's temple.** Three great terraces built for the female pharaoh Hatshepsut beneath the cliffs of Deir el-Bahari.
- **The Colossi of Memnon.** Two giant seated statues of Amenhotep III, all that stands of his vast temple.

## Small group or private {#choose}

- **Small group (GetYourGuide).** About 14 hours to a full day, with hotel pickup in Hurghada and nearby resorts. Its page lists lunch in Luxor, a boat across the Nile and a 30-minute shopping stop; Tutankhamun's tomb is an extra option.
- **Private (Viator).** About 12 to 16 hours, with pickup, for your party alone, so the pace is yours.
- **Small group (Klook).** About 14 hours, from 04:00 to 18:00, in a group of up to 20 (up to 8 per van), with hotel pickup in Hurghada, Sahl Hasheesh, Makadi, El Gouna, Safaga and Soma Bay. Entry tickets are an option you add when booking.
- **Group with entry tickets (Tiqets).** About 14 hours, in a group of up to 35, with hotel pickup. Its page lists entry to the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's temple, the Colossi of Memnon and Karnak, and lunch; Tutankhamun's tomb is not included.

All four offer free cancellation, according to the partners' pages: up to 24 hours before, or up to 12 hours before on the Tiqets option. Check what each includes, especially the entry tickets, before you book.

> **Good to know**
> - It is a very long day: most of it is on the road.
> - Bring water, a hat and sun protection; the Valley of the Kings is a sun trap.
> - Photography rules inside the tombs change. Follow the guards.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Choose this day if you are staying on the Red Sea and want to see Luxor's temples and tombs without moving hotels. If you have more time, a night or two in Luxor lets you see the same sites at an easier pace.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Can I see Luxor in a day from Hurghada?
Yes, the main sites. The options on this page take a full day, from about 12 to 16 hours, with hotel pickup.

?? How long is the drive from Hurghada to Luxor?
About 4.5 hours each way, according to the GetYourGuide option's itinerary.

?? Is Tutankhamun's tomb included?
Not in the standard Valley of the Kings ticket. On the small-group option it is an extra you choose when booking.

?? Small group or private?
A small group shares the day with other travellers and follows a fixed plan. A private trip is for your party alone, at your own pace.
