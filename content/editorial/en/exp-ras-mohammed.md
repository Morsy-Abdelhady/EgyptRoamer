## What it is {#what-it-is}

A half-day or full-day trip from Sharm El Sheikh to **Ras Mohammed National Park**, at the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula. Declared in 1983, it was Egypt's first national park. It protects coral walls, mangroves and desert coast, and it is one of the most celebrated places to snorkel in the Red Sea.

## By road or by boat {#road-or-boat}

- **By road:** a bus or jeep trip into the park, with snorkelling from the shore and stops at its viewpoints and coastal landmarks. A good choice if you prefer to stay close to land.
- **By boat:** a day boat from Sharm reaches the reefs from the water, with time to snorkel at more than one site. Divers can often join the same boat.

> **Park rules**
> Ras Mohammed is protected. Never touch or stand on coral, don't feed fish, and don't collect anything. Rules on anchoring and fishing are strict, and operators should follow them.

## What you see {#what-you-see}

Coral gardens and steep reef walls close to the surface, with reef fish in great numbers. Turtles and rays are seen on some days, but nothing is guaranteed. The contrast of desert and sea is part of the experience.

## What to bring {#what-to-bring}

- Swimwear, a towel and a rash vest or reef-friendly sun protection.
- Your own mask if you have one that fits well.
- Water and a hat. There is little shade on shore.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Snorkellers of every level, including beginners. Tell the operator how well you swim, and ask for a life jacket or snorkel vest if you want one. For diving, see our [Red Sea diving trip](/experiences/red-sea-diving-two-reef-dives/).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Do I need to be a strong swimmer?
No. Guides accompany beginners, and vests are usually available. Say so when you book.

?? Is the park fee included?
It depends on the tour. Check before you book.

?? When is the best time to go?
The sea is warm for most of the year. Spring and autumn combine warm water with comfortable air temperatures. More about the area is in our [Sharm El Sheikh guide](/destinations/sharm/).
