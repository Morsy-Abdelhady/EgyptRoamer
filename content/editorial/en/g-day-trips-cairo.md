Cairo is a good base for more than the city itself. Within a day's reach are the pyramid fields, the Mediterranean and a World Heritage desert of fossil whales. Here are the trips worth a day, and what each one is like.

## Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum {#giza}

The obvious first day. The pyramids and the Sphinx stand on the Giza plateau at the edge of the city, and the Grand Egyptian Museum is close by. Some tours combine both in one long day. Start early for the plateau, and leave the museum for the heat of the afternoon. See our [pyramids of Giza private tour](/experiences/pyramids-of-giza-sphinx-private-tour/).

## Saqqara and Dahshur {#saqqara}

South of Giza, the pyramid fields continue. At **Saqqara** stands the Step Pyramid of Djoser, designed by his architect Imhotep as a pyramid in six stages. At **Dahshur**, the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid of Sneferu rise from open desert. Giza, Saqqara and Dahshur all belong to the World Heritage site *Memphis and its Necropolis – the Pyramid Fields from Giza to Dahshur*, inscribed in 1979. They are far quieter than Giza, and many visitors pair them in one day.

## Alexandria {#alexandria}

Egypt's Mediterranean city, founded by Alexander the Great. A day trip covers the Citadel of Qaitbay on the site of the ancient Pharos, the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, the Roman catacombs of Kom el-Shoqafa and a fish lunch on the Corniche. It's a long day, so go with a car and a plan. See our [Alexandria day trip](/experiences/alexandria-day-trip-from-cairo/). See [our full guide to the Citadel of Qaitbay](/guides/qaitbay-citadel-alexandria/).

## Wadi Al-Hitan (Whale Valley) {#wadi-al-hitan}

In the Fayoum desert, Wadi Al-Hitan holds fossil skeletons of early whales from the time they were changing from land animals to sea mammals; the youngest still show traces of hind legs. UNESCO inscribed it as a World Heritage site in 2005. It's a full day by 4×4, best with a specialist guide.

## Further: the White Desert {#white-desert}

The White and Black Deserts are often sold from Cairo, but they are not a day trip. The point is a night camping among the chalk formations. See our [White Desert safari](/experiences/white-desert-black-desert-safari/).

> **Planning day trips**
> - Start early: traffic leaving Cairo builds through the morning.
> - Check what is included: entry tickets, lunch and a guide vary between tours.
> - Keep one day trip per day. Combining Alexandria with anything else is too much.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Which day trip should I choose first?
Giza and the Grand Egyptian Museum. After that, Saqqara and Dahshur for more pyramids, or Alexandria for a change of scene.

?? Can I do these trips without a tour?
Yes, with a private driver. A guide adds a lot at Saqqara and in Alexandria.

?? Is Wadi Al-Hitan suitable for children?
For older children who enjoy the outdoors, yes. It's a long day in the desert, with little shade.
