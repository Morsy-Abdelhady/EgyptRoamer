## What it is {#what-it-is}

A guided visit to Luxor's West Bank, the burial ground of ancient Thebes. You'll go down into the painted royal tombs of the **Valley of the Kings**, visit the terraced **temple of Hatshepsut** at Deir el-Bahari beneath the cliffs, and usually stop at the **Colossi of Memnon**. It's all part of the UNESCO site *Ancient Thebes with its Necropolis*.

## What you actually do {#what-you-do}

- **Valley of the Kings:** a standard ticket covers a set number of the tombs open that day. The most famous, such as Tutankhamun's, need separate tickets. Inside, long corridors lead down to burial chambers covered in texts and images of the gods, some with their colours remarkably well preserved.
- **Hatshepsut's temple:** three great terraces built for the female pharaoh Hatshepsut, with reliefs of her divine birth and the expedition to the land of Punt.
- **Colossi of Memnon:** two giant seated statues of Amenhotep III, all that stands of his vast temple.

## Why a guide makes a difference {#why-a-guide}

The tombs are dense with symbols and texts that are hard to read without help. A guide explains what you're seeing: the journey through the underworld, the gods, the king's rebirth. They also choose the tombs worth visiting on the day. Guides generally explain everything outside, since talking inside the tombs is limited.

> **Practical notes**
> - **Start early:** the valley is a sun trap by late morning.
> - **Bring** water, a hat and sun protection. Wear comfortable shoes for the descents.
> - **Photography** rules inside the tombs change. Follow the guards.
> - **Small notes** are useful for custodians and toilets.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Everyone visiting Luxor. This is the essential West Bank day. For those with more time or a deeper interest, add Medinet Habu, the Valley of the Queens and the workers' village of Deir el-Medina.

## Combine it with {#combine}

Karnak and Luxor Temple on the East Bank, and a sunrise balloon flight over the West Bank. See our [Luxor guide](/destinations/luxor/) for a three-day plan.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is Tutankhamun's tomb worth the extra ticket?
Its fame is its main draw. The tomb is small, and his treasures are now at the Grand Egyptian Museum in Cairo. Other royal tombs, such as that of Seti I (also a separate ticket), are larger and more richly decorated.

?? How long does the West Bank take?
A morning covers the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's temple and the Colossi of Memnon. A full day allows more tombs and temples.

?? Can I visit without a guide?
Yes, but you'll get much more from the tombs with one, especially on a first visit.
