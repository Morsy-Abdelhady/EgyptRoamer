Wadi Degla is a desert valley on the edge of Cairo, about 10 km from the city. Its walls are made of rock laid down in an ancient sea, and they are full of fossils. This guide is based on the facts given by Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency, which manages the country's protected areas.

## The protected area in brief {#in-brief}

- **Declared:** 1999.
- **Area:** 27.6 km².
- **Type:** natural monument.
- **Where:** Cairo Governorate, about 10 km from Cairo.

## A valley cut through an ancient sea floor {#geology}

Wadi Degla runs from east to west for about 30 km. It passes through limestone that was laid down in a marine environment during the Eocene in the Eastern Desert, about 60 million years ago, so the rock is rich in fossils. The cliffs on either side of the valley are about 50 m high, and a number of side valleys run into it.

Over the ages, rainwater falling from waterfalls onto the limestone has shaped what is called the Degla Canyon, which the Agency compares to the Grand Canyon in the United States.

## Wildlife {#wildlife}

The Agency lists among the valley's animals:

- **Mammals:** gazelles, Nubian ibex, hares, the red fox, the spiny mouse and bats.
- **Insects:** among them dragonflies, ladybirds, tiger moths and antlions.
- **Reptiles:** 18 kinds have been recorded.

## How to visit {#visiting}

- **From Cairo:** about 10 km from the city.
- **Protect the place:** take no fossils or rocks, and leave no rubbish.
- **Entry fees and rules:** they can change, so check with the Environmental Affairs Agency before you go.

> **Bring the basics**
> There is little shade in the valley. Bring water, sun protection and good shoes.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Where is Wadi Degla?
On the edge of Cairo, about 10 km from the city, in Cairo Governorate.

?? How old are the fossils in Wadi Degla?
The limestone they are in was laid down in the sea about 60 million years ago, during the Eocene.

?? Why is it called a canyon?
Rainwater falling from waterfalls onto the limestone has shaped the Degla Canyon over the ages. The Environmental Affairs Agency compares it to the Grand Canyon.

?? What animals live in Wadi Degla?
Among others, gazelles, Nubian ibex, hares and the red fox, and 18 kinds of reptiles.
