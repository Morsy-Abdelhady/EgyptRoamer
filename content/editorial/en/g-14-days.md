Two weeks is enough to see Egypt without rushing: the great sites of Cairo and the Nile Valley, a day on the Mediterranean, and a few days by the sea or in the desert. It builds on our [7-day](/guides/7-days-in-egypt-itinerary/) and [10-day](/guides/10-days-in-egypt-itinerary/) itineraries.

## The plan at a glance {#at-a-glance}

- **Days 1–4: Cairo,** with a day trip to Alexandria.
- **Days 5–6: Aswan,** with Abu Simbel.
- **Days 7–10: the Nile,** by cruise from Aswan to Luxor, or by road with hotel stays.
- **Days 11–14: the Red Sea** at Hurghada, or the Western Desert.

## Day by day {#day-by-day}

+ **Day 1 — Arrive in Cairo** An easy first evening.
+ **Day 2 — Giza** The pyramids early, then the [Grand Egyptian Museum](/guides/grand-egyptian-museum-visit/).
+ **Day 3 — Old Cairo** Coptic and Islamic Cairo, with an evening on Al-Muizz Street. See [Cairo at night](/guides/cairo-at-night/).
+ **Day 4 — Alexandria** A day trip to the Mediterranean city. See our [Alexandria day trip](/experiences/alexandria-day-trip-from-cairo/).
+ **Day 5 — Fly to Aswan** Philae in the afternoon and a felucca at sunset.
+ **Day 6 — Abu Simbel** A pre-dawn start for [Abu Simbel](/experiences/abu-simbel-day-trip-from-aswan/).
+ **Days 7–9 — Down the Nile** A cruise from Aswan to Luxor, with stops at Kom Ombo and Edfu. Or travel by road and stay in hotels. Read [Nile cruise or hotels?](/guides/nile-cruise-or-hotels-luxor-aswan/)
+ **Day 10 — Luxor** [The Valley of the Kings](/experiences/valley-of-the-kings-hatshepsut-temple/) on the West Bank, and [Karnak and Luxor Temple](/guides/karnak-and-luxor-temple/) on the East Bank.
+ **Day 11 — To the Red Sea** Cross from Luxor to Hurghada by road.
+ **Days 12–13 — The reefs** Snorkelling at the Giftun Islands, or a [dive trip](/experiences/red-sea-diving-two-reef-dives/).
+ **Day 14 — Departure** Fly home from Hurghada.

> **Pacing**
> Two weeks gives you room for rest days. Use them: a slow morning in Aswan or an afternoon by the pool after the temple days makes the whole trip better.

## Variations {#variations}

### The desert instead of the sea
Replace days 11–14 with a night in the [White Desert](/guides/white-desert-night/) from Cairo, or several days in [Siwa](/destinations/siwa/) and the [Great Sand Sea](/guides/great-sand-sea/).

### Sharm El Sheikh instead of Hurghada
Fly to Sharm El Sheikh for Ras Mohammed and the Tiran reefs. Read [Hurghada or Sharm El Sheikh?](/guides/snorkelling-hurghada-or-sharm/)

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is two weeks too long for Egypt?
No. It lets you see the main sites at a comfortable pace and add the sea or the desert.

?? Should the Nile cruise go north or south?
Both work. This plan goes north, from Aswan to Luxor, so you can cross from Luxor to the Red Sea.

?? Can I add Siwa and the Red Sea?
In two weeks it's too much. Choose one, or plan a second trip.
