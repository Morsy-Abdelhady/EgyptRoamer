## What it is {#what-it-is}

An evening walk through Cairo's neighbourhoods with a local guide, tasting street food and snacks at small shops, carts and eateries. Cairo eats late, so the city is at its most alive after dark. Tasting as you walk is one of the best ways to understand it.

## What you'll taste {#what-youll-taste}

Stops vary by guide and neighbourhood, but typically include several of these:

- **Koshari:** rice, lentils, pasta, spicy tomato sauce and crispy onions, Cairo's signature dish.
- **Ful medames and ta'meya:** slow-cooked fava beans, and Egyptian falafel made from fava beans.
- **Hawawshi:** spiced minced meat baked inside bread.
- **Feteer meshaltet:** flaky layered pastry, with savoury or sweet fillings.
- **Sweets:** basbousa, konafa, or rice pudding.
- **Drinks:** fresh sugarcane juice, mint tea, or coffee in an *ahwa*.

## Why go with a guide {#why-a-guide}

The best food in Cairo is in places without menus in English, and often without signs at all. A guide knows where to go, orders for you, explains the dishes, and handles hygiene questions. They also add the stories of the neighbourhoods you walk through, often in Downtown or the historic centre.

> **Before you book**
> - Tell the operator about allergies and dietary needs. Vegetarian eating is easy in Egypt, since many classics are meat-free.
> - Come hungry: tastings add up.
> - Wear comfortable shoes: you'll be walking on uneven streets.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

First-time visitors, food lovers, and anyone who wants to see local life beyond the monuments. It's a good first evening in Cairo, when you're still getting your bearings.

## Combine it with {#combine}

A day in Islamic Cairo or Downtown. See our [Cairo guide](/destinations/cairo/) for the areas and more on Cairo food.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is Cairo street food safe to eat?
Eating at busy places with high turnover, as guides do, is the best approach. Drink bottled water, and be careful with raw salads if your stomach is sensitive.

?? Is it suitable for vegetarians?
Yes. Koshari, ful, ta'meya and feteer are traditionally vegetarian. Tell your guide in advance.

?? How late does it run?
Evening tours usually start after sunset and last a few hours. Check the start time with the operator.
