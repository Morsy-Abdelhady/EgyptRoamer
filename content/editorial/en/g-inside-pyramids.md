Standing at the foot of the Great Pyramid is unforgettable. Going inside is a different experience: narrow, steep and hot, but for many people the closest they will ever feel to the builders. This guide explains what you will find inside, so you can decide whether it is for you.

## The pyramids of Giza {#the-pyramids}

Three pyramids of the 4th dynasty stand on the Giza plateau, built for the kings Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure. The oldest and largest, built for Khufu, is the Great Pyramid, completed in the early 25th century BC. Together they were counted among the Seven Wonders of the ancient world, and they are the only one of the wonders still standing. Nearby lies the Great Sphinx, which most scholars date to the reign of Khafre.

## Inside the Great Pyramid {#great-pyramid}

For all its size, the Great Pyramid has very little open space inside. The entrance is on the north side. From there:

- **A descending corridor** runs down through the masonry into the rock below, to an unfinished underground chamber.
- **An ascending passage** branches off it towards the Queen's Chamber and the **Grand Gallery**, a tall corbelled passage about 46 metres long.
- **At the top of the Grand Gallery,** a narrow passage leads to the **King's Chamber**, lined and roofed with granite, with the stone sarcophagus.

Above the King's Chamber are five compartments separated by huge granite slabs, probably built to take the weight of the masonry off the ceiling. You cannot visit them.

> **What you won't see**
> There are no painted walls and no treasure. The pyramids were plundered in ancient and medieval times. The experience is the architecture: the climb, the Grand Gallery and the stillness of the granite chamber.

## Is it for you? {#for-you}

- **Go in** if you are fit and comfortable in tight, steep and warm spaces.
- **Skip it** if you are claustrophobic, have heart or breathing problems, or find stairs and stooping difficult. The view of the pyramids from outside is the highlight anyway.

## Planning your visit {#planning}

- Going inside is usually ticketed separately from the plateau, and which pyramids are open can change. Check on the day.
- Go early, before the heat and the crowds.
- Some tours include entering one of the pyramids. See our [pyramids of Giza tour](/experiences/pyramids-of-giza-sphinx-private-tour/).
- Combine the plateau with the [Grand Egyptian Museum](/guides/grand-egyptian-museum-visit/) in the afternoon.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Can I go inside all three pyramids?
Openings change over time, and some pyramids may be closed. Check which are open when you arrive.

?? Is there anything to see inside?
The passages and the granite King's Chamber. There are no paintings, and the burial goods disappeared long ago.

?? Are photos allowed inside?
Rules change. Follow the guards' instructions.
