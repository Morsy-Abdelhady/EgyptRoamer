Choosing between Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh for snorkelling comes down to the kind of day you want: island stops and an easy combination with Luxor, or reefs around Ras Mohammed and the Straits of Tiran. This guide compares boat and shore options, what beginners can ask an operator, and how to choose without assuming every trip is the same. For dive-site context, see our guide to [the Red Sea's famous dive sites](/guides/red-sea-famous-dive-sites/).

## Hurghada: islands and easy boat trips {#hurghada}

From [Hurghada](/destinations/hurghada/), the classic trip is a boat to the **Giftun Islands**, protected islands offshore with sandy beaches and fringing reefs. Many reefs are shallow enough to be excellent with just a mask and fins. Hurghada is also the Red Sea resort that combines most easily with Luxor and the Nile Valley.

## Sharm El Sheikh: Ras Mohammed and Tiran {#sharm}

From [Sharm El Sheikh](/destinations/sharm/), a well-known option is **Ras Mohammed National Park**, declared in 1983 and known for its coral reefs. The reefs of the **Straits of Tiran** are another day-boat option; conditions vary by site, so ask the operator about that day's route and sea conditions. See our [Ras Mohammed snorkelling trip](/experiences/ras-mohammed-snorkelling-from-sharm/).

## Boat or shore {#boat-or-shore}

- **Boat trips** reach the best offshore reefs and usually stop at two or three sites, often with lunch on board.
- **Shore snorkelling** is possible from some resorts' house reefs and, at Ras Mohammed, from the beach on road trips.
- **Snorkellers and divers** often share the same boat, which suits mixed groups. For diving, see our [Red Sea diving trip](/experiences/red-sea-diving-two-reef-dives/).

## For beginners {#beginners}

You don't need to be a strong swimmer. Most boats provide life jackets or snorkel vests, and guides accompany beginners. Tell the operator your level when you book, and start at a calm, shallow site.

> **Reef etiquette**
> - Never touch, stand on or collect coral or shells.
> - Don't feed fish.
> - Use reef-friendly sun protection, or better, a rash vest.
> - Choose operators who use fixed moorings instead of anchoring on the reef.

## How to choose {#how-to-choose}

- **Choose Hurghada** if you want easy island days and plan to combine the sea with Luxor.
- **Choose Sharm El Sheikh** if the reefs are the main reason for your trip, or if you also want Mount Sinai.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Can I snorkel all year?
Snorkelling trips are offered in different seasons, but water and sea conditions vary. Ask your accommodation or operator about current conditions and suitable clothing for your dates.

?? Do I need my own equipment?
No. Masks, snorkels and fins are usually provided. A mask that fits you well makes a difference, so bring yours if you have one.

?? Will I see turtles or dolphins?
Turtles and rays are seen on many trips, but encounters with any animal depend on the site and the season, and nothing is guaranteed.
