Wadi El-Rayan, in the desert of the Fayoum, is a whole desert world in one protected area: sand dunes, natural springs, wide lakes, plants, wild animals and important marine fossils. This guide is based on the facts given by Egypt's Environmental Affairs Agency, which manages the country's protected areas.

## The protected area in brief {#in-brief}

- **Declared:** 1989.
- **Area:** 1,757.9 km².
- **Type:** protected area for the sustainable use of natural resources.
- **Where:** Fayoum Governorate, about 150 km from Cairo.

The Agency describes the lakes of El-Rayan as a quiet natural environment, free of pollution.

## What is inside {#inside}

- **The waterfalls:** formed where drainage water from farmland collects. They are used for water sports.
- **Ain El-Rayan:** long, dense, moving sand dunes with four natural sulphur springs.
- **Gebel El-Rayan:** deep gorges, known as the "split rock", and a favourite place for a panorama of Wadi El-Rayan and for outings.
- **Wadi Al-Hitan:** a fossil area in the northwest of the protected area, about 40 million years old, with fossil skeletons of primitive whales, shark teeth and shells. Read our [Wadi Al-Hitan guide](/guides/wadi-al-hitan-whale-valley/).

## Wildlife {#wildlife}

Wadi El-Rayan matters because it is a natural home for threatened animals. Around Ain El-Rayan, the Agency lists 16 kinds of desert plants, about 15 kinds of wild mammals, 16 kinds of reptiles and more than 100 kinds of resident and migratory birds.

- **Mammals:** the slender-horned and Dorcas gazelles, the fennec fox, the sand fox, the red fox and the wolf.
- **Birds:** rare migratory birds of prey, among them the peregrine falcon, the saker falcon and the osprey, and ducks, quail and herons.

## How to visit {#visiting}

- **From Cairo:** about 150 km away. Our [Wadi Al-Hitan day trip](/experiences/wadi-al-hitan-day-trip-from-cairo/) crosses the same protected area.
- **Protect the place:** stay on the tracks, take nothing, and leave no rubbish.
- **Entry fees and rules:** they can change, so check with the Environmental Affairs Agency or your operator before you go.

> **Bring the basics**
> There is little shade in the open desert. Bring water, sun protection and good shoes.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Where is Wadi El-Rayan?
In Fayoum Governorate, about 150 km from Cairo.

?? Where do the Wadi El-Rayan waterfalls come from?
They were formed where drainage water from farmland collects.

?? Is Wadi Al-Hitan part of Wadi El-Rayan?
Yes. Wadi Al-Hitan lies in the northwest of the Wadi El-Rayan Protected Area.

?? What animals live in Wadi El-Rayan?
Among others, the slender-horned and Dorcas gazelles, the fennec fox, the sand fox, the red fox and the wolf, and more than 100 kinds of birds.
