Sharm El Sheikh sits at the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula, where the Gulf of Aqaba meets the Red Sea. It's a resort town first and foremost, built for the sea. But it's also the base for two very different experiences: some of the Red Sea's most celebrated reefs at Ras Mohammed and Tiran, and the climb up Mount Sinai to the monastery of St Catherine in the granite mountains inland.

## Why go {#why-go}

- **Ras Mohammed.** Egypt's first national park, protecting the reefs and coast at the tip of Sinai.
- **Famous diving.** The Straits of Tiran, and one of the world's best-known wreck dives, the SS *Thistlegorm*.
- **Mount Sinai and St Catherine.** A sunrise climb and one of the oldest working Christian monasteries, in the *Saint Catherine Area* World Heritage site.

## The sea {#the-sea}

### Ras Mohammed National Park
Declared in 1983 as Egypt's first national park, Ras Mohammed covers the tip of the peninsula and its waters: coral walls, mangroves and desert coast. It can be visited by boat for snorkelling and diving, or by road for its beaches and viewpoints. Rules on anchoring, fishing and collecting are strict.

### The Straits of Tiran
A chain of reefs in the strait off Sharm, known for strong currents and big marine life. It's a classic day-boat destination.

### The SS Thistlegorm
A British supply ship sunk during the Second World War, lying with its cargo of vehicles and equipment still aboard. The dive is for certified divers and is usually done as a long day trip or from a liveaboard.

Our [Red Sea diving trip](/experiences/red-sea-diving-two-reef-dives/) is a good introduction to the reefs with a guide.

> **Diving and snorkelling responsibly**
> Choose licensed operators, follow the park rules, and never touch or stand on coral. Currents in the strait can be strong, so dive within your experience level.

## Mount Sinai and St Catherine {#mount-sinai}

Inland, the granite mountains of South Sinai hold **St Catherine's Monastery** at the foot of **Mount Sinai**, sacred to Christians, Jews and Muslims. The classic visit is an overnight climb to watch the sunrise from the summit, then a visit to the monastery when it opens.

- The summit is cold before dawn in every season. Bring warm layers, a torch and water.
- Monastery opening days and times are limited and change. Check before you go.
- It's a long excursion from Sharm, and staying overnight in the St Catherine area makes it easier.

## Naama Bay and town life {#naama-bay}

**Naama Bay** is the lively centre, with its promenade, restaurants and bars. The **Old Market** in the old town has spices and souvenirs. Most hotels spread along the coast in self-contained resorts. Many have their own reefs reached from a jetty.

## Dahab and beyond {#dahab}

Up the coast, **Dahab** is Sinai's laid-back alternative, with low-rise cafés on the water, windsurfing and famous shore diving. Its best-known site, the Blue Hole, is spectacular for snorkellers at the surface. The deep dives there are dangerous and only for highly experienced technical divers.

## How long, and when to go {#when-to-go}

**How long.** Four to six days leaves time for a Ras Mohammed trip, a Tiran or Thistlegorm dive day, the Mount Sinai excursion and rest.

**When to go.**
- **Spring and autumn** are ideal.
- **Winter** is pleasant on the coast but cold in the mountains at night.
- **Summer** is hot.
- The sea stays warm for most of the year.

## Getting there {#getting-there}

- Sharm El Sheikh International Airport has flights from Cairo and many European cities, and most visitors fly in.
- Most resorts include transfers.
- Mount Sinai and Dahab are reached by road with a driver or on an organised excursion.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is Sharm El Sheikh good for beginners?
Yes. Many resorts have calm house reefs, and dive centres run try-dives and courses. Ras Mohammed can be enjoyed by snorkellers too.

?? Can I visit Mount Sinai from Sharm in one trip?
Yes, as an overnight excursion to climb for sunrise. It's tiring. Staying a night in the St Catherine area makes it more comfortable.

?? Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada?
Sharm has Ras Mohammed, Tiran and Mount Sinai on its doorstep. [Hurghada](/destinations/hurghada/) combines more easily with [Luxor](/destinations/luxor/) and the Nile Valley.

?? What else can I combine with Sharm?
A flight to [Cairo](/destinations/cairo/) for the pyramids and museums is the most common pairing.
