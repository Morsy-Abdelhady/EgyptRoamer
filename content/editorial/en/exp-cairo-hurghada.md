## What it is {#what-it-is}

A full day from your hotel on the Red Sea to Cairo, with a flight each way. You see the **Pyramids of Giza** and the **Great Sphinx**, and one of Cairo's two great museums of ancient Egypt: the **Grand Egyptian Museum** in Giza or the **Egyptian Museum** on Tahrir Square. You choose the museum when you book.

## The day {#the-day}

- **The flights.** Pickup at your hotel, a transfer to Hurghada airport and a flight to Cairo. The outbound flight takes about 80 minutes, according to the GetYourGuide option's itinerary.
- **The pyramids.** The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the only surviving Wonder of the Ancient World. Beside it stand the pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure.
- **The Sphinx.** The Great Sphinx lies below the pyramids, by the valley temple.
- **The museum.** Britannica describes the Grand Egyptian Museum as the largest archaeological museum in the world; its collection spans the predynastic period to the Greco-Roman era. The Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square opened in 1902.

## Group or private {#choose}

- **Group (GetYourGuide).** About 16 to 17 hours, with hotel pickup. Its page lists the flights, lunch with views of the pyramids, an Egyptologist guide and a 30-minute shopping stop; a Nile boat trip and entry into a pyramid cost extra.
- **Private (Viator).** About 8 to 14 hours, with pickup, for your party alone. Its itinerary covers the pyramids and the Sphinx, the Egyptian Museum (or the Grand Egyptian Museum as an option) and Khan el-Khalili.

Both offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before, according to the partners' pages. Check what each includes, especially the flights and the entry tickets, before you book.

> **Good to know**
> - It is a very long day, with two flights.
> - Bring your passport or ID card, as the GetYourGuide option asks.
> - The order of the visits can change with the flight times and Cairo's traffic.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Choose this day if you are staying on the Red Sea and want to see the pyramids without moving hotels. With more time, a few nights in Cairo let you see Giza, the museums and Historic Cairo at an easier pace.
