The Grand Egyptian Museum, in Giza, is the new home of ancient Egypt's treasures. Britannica describes it as the largest archaeological museum in the world. Its collection spans thousands of years, from the predynastic period to the Greco-Roman era. This guide explains what to expect and how to fit it into your days in Cairo.

## What the museum is {#what-it-is}

The museum brings together objects from institutions across Egypt, including the Egyptian Museum in downtown Cairo. It was designed by the Dublin firm Heneghan Peng. The building's facade, with its pattern of triangles, and its sloping interior ceilings echo the pyramids nearby. The official opening was on 1 November 2025, and it opened to the public on 4 November 2025.

## What you will see {#what-you-will-see}

### The atrium and the grand staircase
You enter through a facade of translucent alabaster into a shaded atrium, where a colossal statue of Ramesses II, more than 3,200 years old, greets visitors. From there a grand staircase, lined with statues of gods and kings, climbs to the exhibition halls. Tall windows at the southeast end of the building look out towards the pyramids.

### The galleries
The displays are arranged by period and by themes such as religion, kingship and society, so you can follow the story of Egypt through time.

### Tutankhamun
For the first time, the full collection from the tomb of Tutankhamun is shown together. For many visitors it is the reason to come. The tomb itself is in Luxor; read about it in our [Valley of the Kings guide](/guides/valley-of-the-kings-tombs-and-tickets/).

> **Plan your time**
> The museum is very large. Decide in advance what matters most to you, for example the staircase, Tutankhamun and one period, rather than trying to see everything.

## With the pyramids {#with-the-pyramids}

The museum and the Giza pyramids are close to each other, so many visitors combine them in one day: the plateau early, before the heat, and the museum in the afternoon. Some tours include both with an Egyptologist guide. See our [pyramids of Giza tour](/experiences/pyramids-of-giza-sphinx-private-tour/), and our guide [Inside the pyramids of Giza](/guides/inside-the-pyramids-of-giza/).

## Practical tips {#tips}

- Check opening times and ticket options on the museum's official channels before you go. They can change.
- A guide makes a big difference in the galleries.
- Wear comfortable shoes. Distances inside are long.
- Leave time for the windows and terraces facing the pyramids.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? How much time do I need at the Grand Egyptian Museum?
At least half a day. Enthusiasts can easily spend longer.

?? Is the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir still worth visiting?
Many objects moved to the new museum, including the Tutankhamun collection. If your time is short, the Grand Egyptian Museum comes first.

?? Can I combine it with the pyramids in one day?
Yes. Start at the pyramids in the morning and spend the afternoon in the museum.
