## What it is {#what-it-is}

A 4×4 excursion from Siwa into the **Great Sand Sea**, one of the largest dune fields in the world, stretching south of the oasis. With a local driver you cross towering dunes, stop to swim in springs in the middle of the desert, try sand-boarding, and watch the sun set over the sand.

## What the day is like {#what-its-like}

Trips usually leave in the afternoon, so you're in the dunes in the best light:

- **Dune driving:** steep climbs and descents that are part rollercoaster, part landscape tour. Tell your driver if you'd prefer a gentler ride.
- **Springs:** hot and cold springs in the desert. A dip in warm water with dunes all around is the highlight for many.
- **Sand-boarding** down the dunes.
- **Sunset** from the top of a dune, often with tea made on a small fire.

## Choosing an operator {#choosing}

> **What to check**
> - A licensed Siwan operator and an experienced desert driver. Never go into the dunes on your own.
> - A vehicle in good condition, with water and communication equipment.
> - Whether permits for the desert areas are arranged for you.
> - Whether the trip includes springs, sand-boarding and sunset, or goes further for a longer day or an overnight camp.

## What to bring {#what-to-bring}

Sun protection, sunglasses and water; swimwear and a towel for the springs; and a warm layer, because it gets cold quickly after sunset, especially in winter.

## Who it suits {#who-it-suits}

Anyone staying in Siwa. It's the classic excursion. The dune driving can be rough, so it's not suitable if you have back problems or are pregnant. Ask for a calmer route.

## Combine it with {#combine}

The Shali fortress, the Oracle of Amun and Cleopatra's Spring in the oasis itself. See our [Siwa guide](/destinations/siwa/).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

?? Is the dune driving scary?
It can be exhilarating. Drivers adjust to their passengers, so just ask for a gentler ride.

?? Do I need to book in advance?
In the main season it's wise to arrange it on arrival or through your lodge. Local operators run trips most days.

?? Can I stay overnight in the desert?
Yes, many operators offer an overnight camp in the dunes, for a night under the stars.
