# Link outreach (priority 3 of `docs/PLAN-2026-10-30.md`)

Goal: 5–10 real referring sites by Feb 2027. No bought links, guest-post fees or link swaps.

1. **Find** (free): journalist requests on Qwoted and Featured (owner's accounts), travel blogs and resource pages
   that link to Egypt guides ("Egypt travel resources", "Egypt itinerary" + "useful links"), university or museum
   pages on Egyptology, forums only where a helpful answer fits. Check the owner's profile expertise is
   Travel / Egypt.
2. **Check fit**: real site with traffic signs, topic matches a page we have, no paid-link or spam signs.
3. **Prepare**: for each prospect, the exact page of ours that helps their reader, and a short draft. Many reporters
   refuse AI text: the owner writes every reply in his own words, so drafts are notes and facts, not finished text.
4. **Log** in `growth/outreach/prospects.csv` (site, page, contact route, our page, status, date) and, after the owner
   sends, in `growth/outreach/outreach-log.csv`.
5. Never send, submit forms or create accounts. Report the week's list to the owner in Egyptian Arabic.
