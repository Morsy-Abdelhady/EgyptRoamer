# Monthly growth report

1. Sum the month's rows in `docs/KPI-WEEKLY.md`, and read the full calendar month in Search Console, GA4, Bing and
   every partner dashboard. Bookings and revenue come only from the partners.
2. Save `growth/reports/monthly/YYYY-MM.md` and add one row to `growth/analytics/monthly-performance.csv`.
3. Sections: summary; against the month's checkpoint in `docs/PLAN-2026-10-30.md` (on track, a little below, far
   below); search (clicks, impressions, indexed pages, top queries); affiliate (clicks per partner, bookings,
   commission); content published or refreshed and its results; links gained (`growth/outreach/outreach-log.csv`);
   social (which channel brought visits; Pinterest stays only if it brings visits after two months); spend
   (`growth/management/budget-log.csv`); what worked, what did not and why; next month's 3 priorities.
4. Keep actual numbers apart from `[Estimate]`s. No daily Google visits by March 2027 means the plan needs rethinking
   with the owner: say so.
5. Record any change of direction in `growth/management/decision-log.md`. Report to the owner in Egyptian Arabic.
