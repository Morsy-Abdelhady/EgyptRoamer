Affiliate language

View Deal · Check Availability · Compare Options · Explore Tours · View Cruise · See Hotel · Book with Partner. Always show “via Partner”.

One gold per view

Gold marks the single most important action in a viewport. Secondary actions are outline or ghost.

Never

Fake booking flows, countdown timers, “only 1 left” pressure, or gold backgrounds on whole sections.

04 · Iconography

One thin-stroke family

24px grid, 1.4 stroke, round joins. Architecture and geography are referenced abstractly — no hieroglyph clip-art.

05 · Principles

How it should feel

Editorial first

Big serif headlines, generous whitespace, photography at full bleed. Commerce lives inside the story, never above it.

Motion with meaning

Motion carries the journey: scale, masks, parallax and crossfades. Nothing loops for attention. Reduced motion is respected.

Consistent grade

Photos get a light desaturate + contrast lift and a warm-to-teal tint that follows the journey.

Avoid

Hieroglyph wallpaper, pyramid overload, crowded card grids, cheap gradients, stock “tourist” clichés.
