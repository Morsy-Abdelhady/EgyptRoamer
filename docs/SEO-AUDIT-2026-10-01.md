Scope: technical, international, structured data, internal linking, content readiness and the indexing switch. Sources: the code (Core `includes/seo.php`, `multilingual.php`, `cli.php`; theme `inc/setup.php`, `inc/template-tags.php`), the rendered 233-URL crawl (`tools/qa/site-crawl.mjs`, 8 languages), anonymous production HTML, and a full local launch simulation (`tools/qa/launch-sim.py`). ## 5. Sitemap
- WordPress core sitemaps, split per language by Polylang: pages, destinations, experiences, and the hubs.
- Only published items ticked "Ready to index" are listed. An empty Journal, "Uncategorized" and users are excluded.
- **Fixed:** the indexable hubs (`/destinations/`, `/experiences/`, later `/guides/` etc.) were missing, because WordPress lists no post-type archives. A Core provider now lists each hub per language while it has an indexable item. Its lastmod is the newest item's real modification date.
- **Launch simulation:** 176 URLs (22 per language), unique, all 200, none noindex, all self-canonical, hreflang complete and reciprocal. 0 failures.

## 6. Canonical
Every page except search is self-canonical in its own language: 225 of 233 crawled URLs; search intentionally has none. Translations are never canonicalized to English. Archive canonicals drop every query string (filters, `utm_*`, cache busters).

## 7. Hreflang
Every translated group lists all 8 languages plus `x-default` (English). Reciprocity was verified on all 176 launch URLs, with every alternate answering 200 in its own language. Empty archives carry no hreflang, because Polylang omits them; those archives are noindex.

## 12. Content quality (index readiness)
The word-count rule was **removed** (owner instruction). `wp egypt-roamer index` now judges every published destination and experience in every language on:
- **body and its own description:** both present;
- **completeness against the English original:** same H2 sections, FAQ items and list items, and at least as many in-content links (a shortened translation fails);
- **in-content links:** at least one (no dead ends);
- **language integrity:** for Arabic, Russian and Chinese, at least 60% of the letters must be in the language's script, which catches untranslated English.

**Result: 120 of 120 pass.**
- **The "short" pages:** the 4 Russian and Chinese experience pages that the old 300-word count flagged are complete translations. They have the same 7 sections, FAQs and links as English; the word counter misread Chinese and Russian.
- **Proof that the checks can fail:** a half-length Chinese body drops from 7 sections to 3, and English text on a Chinese page scores 0% Chinese characters.
- **Translations:** all 105 translated editorial files are marked `review: approved` and current against their English source.

**Editorial assessment (English):**
- **Destinations (670–1,890 words): strong.** Areas, when to go, how to get there, how long to stay, and FAQs, sourced in `docs/CONTENT-SOURCES.md`.
- **Experiences (410–580 words): complete for their narrow intent.** What it is, what the day is like, who it suits, what to check before booking, combinations and FAQs. They don't use thin affiliate copy; there are no offers yet.

## 23. Launch blockers and the switch
**Blockers outside SEO:** Terms and legal review, email MX, the HTML cache TTL (O2), and the owner's go-ahead.

**The switch (owner, production, in this order):**
1. SSH:
   ```
   cd ~/html && wp egypt-roamer index
   ```
   This is the review. Expect 120 "would tick".
2. If the review looks right:
   ```
   wp egypt-roamer index --apply
   ```
3. wp-admin → Settings → Reading → untick "Discourage search engines" → Save.
4. GoDaddy → Flush Cache.
5. Verify:
   - Run `python tools/qa/launch-sim.py https://egyptroamer.com` **once**: about 400 requests. If Cloudflare challenges it, stop and use the samples below instead.
   - Or check by hand: `/robots.txt`, `/wp-sitemap.xml`, and one page per language (`<meta name="robots">` without noindex).
6. PageSpeed Insights and the Rich Results Test on: the homepage, `/destinations/cairo/`, `/ar/destinations/القاهرة/` and `/experiences/abu-simbel-day-trip-from-aswan/`.
7. Search Console:
   - Verify the domain property (DNS TXT).
   - Submit `https://egyptroamer.com/wp-sitemap.xml`.
   - Use URL Inspection on the same 4 pages and request indexing for those and the 8 homepages only.

**Rollback:** tick "Discourage search engines" again. Every page returns to noindex at once.

## 24. Post-launch monitoring
- **Week 1:** Page indexing (indexed / excluded, and why), the canonical Google chose, hreflang errors (International targeting), sitemap read status, crawl errors.
- **Weeks 2–4:** impressions, clicks, CTR and queries per country and language; top landing pages; pages "Crawled – currently not indexed" (treat these as a content-quality signal, not a technical one).
- Don't read early traffic swings as success or failure.
