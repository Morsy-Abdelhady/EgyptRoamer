# Project review and recommendations: 15 Oct 2026

The basis for this review:

- **Pages reviewed:** the full UX pass of 15 Oct, in the browser, at 1366/1440 px and 390 px. It covered every destination, experience and guide in 8 languages, plus the home planner and the assistant.
- **Search data:** Search Console, 28 days, data up to 12 Oct.
- **First-party Visits:** recording since 15 Oct.

## Where the project stands

| Area | State |
|---|---|
| Product | 7 destinations, 8 experiences and 4 guides, each in 8 languages: 19 pages per language. |
| Monetisation | 10 verified Viator offers. Each destination and experience has a booking action within one tap (theme 1.2.51). |
| Demand | 37 impressions and 2 clicks in 28 days, average position 38.7. Indexable only since 2 Oct, and GSC validation started on 15 Oct. |
| Measurement | First-party page views, `/go/` redirect log, Viator dashboard (by `campaign=er-{lang}-{placement}`). No weekly KPI view yet. |
| Main constraint | Traffic, not the interface. With 19 pages per language there is little to rank. |

## Recommendations, in priority order

### P0: this week

1. **One weekly KPI sheet**, every Monday. It answers one question: did the funnel move this week?
   - The funnel: impressions → clicks (GSC + Bing) → page views → partner taps → Viator clicks → bookings → commission.
   - Without it, design changes cannot be judged.
2. **Release QA that covers every template.** Today's misses (Alexandria with no booking, the narrow guides) came from testing sample pages only. One automated check would run before each release, on every published page in all 8 languages. It would confirm:
   - a booking action exists;
   - the title and location are translated;
   - the image loads;
   - the layout type matches the template;
   - there are no JS errors.
3. **Finish the search set-up.**
   - Bing: submit the remaining 49 URLs on 16 Oct.
   - GSC: recheck on 22 Oct.
   - Domain property: Search Console only gives access to the URL-prefix property, not the domain one (`sc-domain:`). That is fine for now. Do not start a DNS verification flow by accident.

### P1: the next 2–6 weeks (growth)

4. **Content is the growth lever.**
   - **Pace:** 2 guides a week, each answering a search with booking intent and linked to a verified offer.
   - **Order:**
     1. Abu Simbel from Aswan: options and timing;
     2. Luxor–Aswan Nile cruise or hotels;
     3. Day trips from Cairo, including Alexandria;
     4. Snorkelling in Hurghada or Sharm;
     5. White Desert overnight: what to expect.
   - **Languages:** Arabic gets equal priority. The first queries with impressions are Arabic ("اسوان", "سيوة فين في مصر").
5. **2–3 verified offers per experience** instead of 1 (private, small group, budget). A choice converts better than a single option, and Viator's landing page already shows the competition.
   - Same verification rules.
   - Owner approval per offer.
6. **Experience pages for Alexandria and Sharm.** These two have destination pages only, while the other destinations each have a bookable experience page.
7. **Trust (E-E-A-T).**
   - A real About page and a named editor or author.
   - "How we choose partners" linked from the booking boxes (the link exists).
   - "Reviewed on" dates on guides.

### P2: later, owner decisions

8. **A second partner** (e.g. GetYourGuide) for comparison and resilience. Today all revenue depends on one programme and its landing-page choices. Only verified programmes and URLs.
9. **Own photography.**
   - Every page still uses hot-linked Unsplash stand-ins.
   - Featured images reduce that risk and set the site apart.
10. **Speed check** (PageSpeed / Core Web Vitals) on the home, experience and guide templates, mobile first. This is not measured yet.
11. **Email.** The subscriber form exists. A useful reason to subscribe would be the 7-day itinerary as a printable page built from the existing guide.

## Risks to watch

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Viator lands affiliate traffic on its "Explore {city}" page (by design) | Track Viator conversion per campaign. Consider a second partner (rec. 8). |
| Thin content inventory | Weekly publishing plan (rec. 4). |
| Hot-linked stand-in photos | Featured images (rec. 9). |
| Releases tested on sample pages only | Full-template QA (rec. 2). |
| AI-reviewed translations | Native-speaker spot check of top pages per language, once traffic exists. |
