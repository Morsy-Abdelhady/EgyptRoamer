**`tools/qa/seo-audit.py` (new)** fetches every URL without following redirects:
- the full inventory (`site-inventory.php`, 233 URLs);
- every URL in every XML sitemap;
- 42 probe URLs: utility, duplicate, legacy and edge cases.

For each page it reads:
- status, `X-Robots-Tag`, meta robots;
- canonical, hreflang, `lang`/`dir`;
- title, description, Open Graph;
- H1–H3 outline;
- JSON-LD;
- internal links and images.

Then it checks the site as a whole:
- indexability and sitemap;
- the canonical/hreflang matrix and reciprocity;
- metadata uniqueness and length;
- headings;
- schema validity and consistency with the visible content;
- internal links (status, redirects, noindex targets, wrong language, orphans, click depth);
- images, robots.txt, affiliate and chat routes.

## 8. International SEO
Checked in all 8 languages for every indexable page:
- localized title, description, H1, breadcrumbs and JSON-LD names;
- `lang`, and `dir="rtl"` for Arabic;
- `og:locale`;
- own-language internal links;
- sitemap per language;
- hreflang matrix.

**Fixed: slugs cut mid-word.**
- WordPress caps a slug at 200 bytes, and an encoded Arabic or Cyrillic letter takes 4–6 of them:
  - `…/وأبي-الهو/` ("…and the Sphin");
  - `…/بسيارات-الدفع-الربا/`;
  - `…/к-пирамида/`;
  - `…/на-4x4-и-горячие-и/`.
- **Core 1.2.18:**
  - new posts never end mid-word or on a dangling connector;
  - `wp egypt-roamer slugs` shortens existing ones (old slugs keep redirecting, 301 in one hop: verified locally).
