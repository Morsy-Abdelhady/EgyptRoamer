# Desktop trip pages, proposal 3: trip worlds (2026-10-16)

**Owner's brief:** a stunning, one-of-a-kind design. For antiquities, for example, a background of pharaonic inscriptions, so the customer lives the trip before starting it, with photos or short videos. A stunning presentation for every trip.

- **Published:** https://claude.ai/artifact/2zzhquCDFh3rW6AMyfgy3h. Proposal 1 is at https://claude.ai/artifact/SdbGnRYkXTKQDyMsA73cJG, proposal 2 at https://claude.ai/artifact/4y7hqAxdKHubDFabkoLxQe.
- **Antiquities world, Valley of the Kings (full page):**
  - a carved wall of hieroglyph columns lit by a torch that follows the pointer, over the trip's photo;
  - the title rising word by word;
  - "Down the corridor": the three stops told as the reader scrolls, the picture changing, a corridor line lighting each stop. The page's own text says "long corridors lead down to burial chambers".
  - "The walls speak. A guide reads them": the practical notes on carved tablets;
  - who it suits and combine it with, on papyrus;
  - "Choose your way in": the three options as large cards, the comparison, hotels and the disclosure;
  - a "Book this trip" capsule on screen throughout.
- **Openers for three other worlds:**
  - Sea (Red Sea diving): caustic light and rising bubbles.
  - Desert (White and Black Desert): stars coming out and drifting sand.
  - Nile (dinner cruise): city lights trembling on the water.
  - Each opener carries the trip's real intro, a line from its page, its facts and its three real options.
- **Rules kept:**
  - The hieroglyphs are decoration in Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs. No text or translation is claimed.
  - All words and facts come from the live pages.
  - Photos are each trip's own approved photo (`imgs3.mjs`), never a partner's.
  - Motion comes from the photos plus canvas light, stars and sand; there is no video.
- **Open decision:** short real videos need an approved free source, for example Pexels, under the same rule that the location tag names the exact place.
