## Audience

Most visitors to Egypt book package holidays, especially for the Red Sea. Our market is the independent traveller who
plans the trip: Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, Nile cruises, multi-day tours, and day trips from the resorts.

## Priorities, in order

1. **Earn more per booking.** Lead with the higher-value products the partners already sell: Nile cruises,
   multi-day tours and hotels. A higher average commission lowers the number of bookings needed.
2. **Raise the booking rate.** One comparison page for each popular trip: the partner offers we already list, how they
   differ (private or group, duration and pickup, from the partner's page only), and clear booking buttons. No prices,
   ratings or reviews that we write ourselves.
3. **Links, every week.** This is the biggest factor in how fast Google traffic grows. Claude lists sites and prepares
   draft replies; the owner sends every message. Goal: 5–10 real referring sites by Feb 2027.
4. **Pre-trip pages for traffic.** 10–15 pages from official sources only: visa, safety, best time to visit, sample
   itineraries and getting between cities. Each page links to the matching comparison pages.
5. **No new place guides at the old pace.** Improve existing pages that start to rank instead.
6. **Social:** daily posting continues. Keep Pinterest only if it brings visits after two months.
