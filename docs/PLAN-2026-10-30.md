## Break-even [Estimate]

About 10–14 bookings a month, assuming an average commission of $4–6. That means roughly 5,000–12,000 visits a
month, at the usual affiliate rate of 1–3 bookings per 1,000 visits; this is an industry figure, not our own data.
Replace these with real numbers from Search Console and the partner dashboards as soon as there are some.

## Audience

Most visitors to Egypt book package holidays, especially for the Red Sea. Our market is the independent traveller who
plans the trip: Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, and day trips from the resorts.

## Work, Nov 2026 – Feb 2027

1. **Content:** no new place guides at the old pace. Write 10–15 pre-trip pages from official sources only: visa,
   safety, best time to visit, sample itineraries and getting between cities. Improve the day-trip pages that earn
   commission.
2. **Links (the main lever):** Claude lists sites and prepares draft replies; the owner sends every message. Goal:
   5–10 real referring sites by February.
3. **Social:** daily posting continues. Keep Pinterest only if it brings visits after two months.
4. **Review every two weeks:** Search Console impressions, clicks and pages starting to rank, plus partner clicks and
   bookings. Improve the pages that start ranking.
