# UI audit on real screens, 20 Oct 2026 (themes 1.3.2–1.3.3)

Owner: "although you said all was fine, many UI problems remain; should I tell you or will you find them?" I looked
at screenshots taken while scrolling the live and local site at 1920, 1440, 820 (tablet) and 390 (phone), in
English and Arabic. Earlier passes used the prototype's 1048px frame and full-page shots, which hid these problems.

## Found and fixed
| # | Where | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trip pages, wide screens | Sections started 56px from the left while header and footer are centred; at 1920 the right third was empty | Sections line up with the site container and use its width (1.3.2) |
| 2 | Trip opener | The photo stopped ~60px short of the right edge (img max-width capped the 106% zoom) | `max-width:none` (1.3.2) |
| 3 | Trip page, phones | The journey photo was 640px tall, so a black band showed under it | Fills the screen (1.3.2) |
| 4 | Trips & tours, ≥1200px | Full-width place banner over a 4-column row with 1–3 trips: half-empty rows | Banner as the first column, trips beside it: one row per place (1.3.2) |
| 5 | Arabic Trips & tours | Nile cruise, Alexandria day trip and Ras Mohammed under "More trips"; no Alexandria group | Trip without a place takes another language copy's place (1.3.2) |
| 6 | Touch screens | Breadcrumbs, filter toggles, "Read the … guide", "About {place}", journey stop names, "Skip to booking", "Compare", "All … trips" were 16–24px tall | 44px hit area (1.3.2) |
| 7 | Guides and place pages, tablets (601–900px) | Booking box drops below the article and there was no booking bar: no Book button while reading | The phone's booking bar shows on tablets (1.3.3) |
| 8 | Trips & tours, phones | "Best time Oct – Apr" broke in the middle | Own line (1.3.2) |

Checked and not bugs: the empty mood images on the homepage (loaded only when that mood is picked); "Website" in
English on Arabic pages (hidden spam-trap fields).

## Not changed (judgement, open to the owner)
- Hotels page: cards have uneven heights (each place has 1–4 areas).
- Trips filter bar wraps to two lines at 1920 (checkboxes on the second line).
- Trips page on phones is long (11 trips, one card per row).

QA after 1.3.3: `page-coverage` on production, 344 pages × 8 languages, all ok.
