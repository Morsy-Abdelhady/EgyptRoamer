# UI audit on real screens, 20 Oct 2026 (themes 1.3.2–1.3.3)

Owner: "although you said all was fine, many UI problems remain; should I tell you or will you find them?" I looked
at screenshots taken while scrolling the live and local site at 1920, 1440, 820 (tablet) and 390 (phone), in
English and Arabic. Earlier passes used the prototype's 1048px frame and full-page shots, which hid these problems.

## Found and fixed
| # | Where | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Trip opener | The photo stopped ~60px short of the right edge (img max-width capped the 106% zoom) | `max-width:none` (1.3.2) |

**What it flags, and what was done (theme 1.3.16):**
- **Accessibility, fixed** (axe-core run on 7 pages: 0 violations after the fix):
  - small gold text at 3.1:1 on the light background; the `--gold-deep` token is now 60% gold (4.8:1), also in the guide and trip pages' local copies;
  - the large "How it works" numbers in the darker gold (3.4:1, enough for large text);
  - the footer company line at 4.5:1;
  - the papyrus labels in a darker clay (5.4:1);
  - Viator's chip text on the trip page in white;
  - the trip filter chips: links that act as toggle buttons get `role="button"` (Space works too); the Travel Guide's place chips are plain links and use `aria-current`;
  - the guide breadcrumb is no longer `aria-hidden` (it held links);
  - the guide's trip card title is an `h3` (an `h4` skipped a level);
  - the trip page's booking box no longer repeats the section's landmark label.
- **Render-blocking CSS, not changed:** each page has one stylesheet (`bundle-*.css`, about 180 KB, served with Brotli and cached 31 days). Its estimated cost on slow 4G is 0.8–1.75 s, the round trip itself. The fix is inlining the first screen's CSS and loading the rest later. That's a bigger change with a risk of an unstyled flash, so it's left for its own release.
- **Image delivery, not changed:** no image is oversized at the phone's pixel density (measured on `/experiences/`, 23 images). The estimated savings come from the format and compression of the hot-linked Unsplash files, which the site doesn't control.
- **LCP** stays at about 3.5 s, with the hero photo from images.unsplash.com.
