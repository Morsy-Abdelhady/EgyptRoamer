# Blocked guides — data requirements — 2026-10-03

These three guides stay unwritten until the inputs below exist. The site's hard rule is no invented prices, schedules,
safety statistics, operator claims or fees. Each input needs a **source** and a **date checked**, recorded in
`docs/CONTENT-SOURCES.md`.

## 1. "Is Egypt Safe for Tourists? An Honest 2026 Guide" (`is-egypt-safe-for-tourists`)

DATA NEEDED
- Current travel-advice status for Egypt from at least two governments, and the regions each advises against
  (Western Desert border areas, North Sinai, the Libyan and Sudanese borders), with the date of each advisory.
- Practical safety items that can be stated without statistics: tourist police, checkpoints on intercity roads,
  guided-convoy rules where they apply, scams and touts at major sites, taxi/ride-hailing practice, women travellers,
  dress norms, health (heat, water, sun), and emergency numbers.

SOURCE NEEDED
- Official advisories: UK FCDO "Egypt travel advice", US State Department "Egypt Travel Advisory", optionally Germany
  (Auswärtiges Amt) and Canada. All of them are dated pages.
- Emergency numbers: an official Egyptian government source.
- Health: WHO or CDC traveller pages.

EDITORIAL WORK NEEDED
- A clear statement of when it was last reviewed, with a review cadence (the advisories change).
- No crime or incident statistics unless an official dataset with a year is cited.
- The title says "2026": keep it only if the guide is reviewed during 2026.
- Internal links: Cairo guide, the destination pages for the regions covered, the Trip assistant / contact page.

## 2. "The Best Nile Cruises, Compared Cabin by Cabin" (`best-nile-cruises`)

DATA NEEDED
- The actual vessels to compare: names, operators, category (standard cruise ship, boutique ship, dahabiya), number
  of cabins, cabin types, and cabin sizes where published.
- Itineraries: Luxor–Aswan, Aswan–Luxor or Lake Nasser, the number of nights (3/4/7), and which temples are visited.
- What is included: meals, guiding, entry fees. Season and departure days only if the operator publishes them.
- Which of these vessels Egypt Roamer will actually link to (affiliate partner and URL). A comparison page without a
  partner path is a dead end.

SOURCE NEEDED
- Each operator's official site or deck plans (dated), or the affiliate partner's product data.
- No prices unless they come from a live partner feed or a dated official rate. "From $X" figures copied from
  aggregators are not acceptable.

EDITORIAL WORK NEEDED
- The "cabin by cabin" promise only holds if real cabin data exists. Otherwise retitle it, e.g. "How to Choose a Nile
  Cruise", which can be written from the qualitative facts already sourced: route, nights, Edfu and Kom Ombo stops,
  cruise vs dahabiya.
- Links: Luxor, Aswan, the 7-days guide (cruise variation), and Nile cruise offers once they are live.

## 3. "What a Trip to Egypt Really Costs in 2026" (`egypt-travel-costs`)

DATA NEEDED
- Entry fees for the main sites (Giza plateau, Grand Egyptian Museum, Karnak, Valley of the Kings, Abu Simbel,
  Philae), in EGP, for foreign visitors.
- Visa options and fees (e-visa, on arrival) for the main nationalities.
- Transport: domestic flights (typical range, with route and date), the sleeper train, ride-hailing in Cairo.
- Accommodation and food bands, defined as ranges with a source and a date, or left out.
- The exchange-rate date used for any USD/EUR conversion.

SOURCE NEEDED
- Entry fees: the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities (egymonuments.gov.eg) ticket pages.
- Visa: the official Egypt e-visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg).
- Train: Egyptian National Railways or the sleeper-train operator's official site.
- Anything else: the affiliate partners' live prices, or the owner's own dated receipts.

EDITORIAL WORK NEEDED
- Prices date quickly. Show "prices checked: <month year>" and schedule a quarterly review, or the page will mislead.
- The homepage trip-builder budget bands (`budget_smart/comfort/luxury`) are empty for the same reason. Fill them
  from the same dated sources, or leave them hidden.
- Links: the 7-days guide, the best-time guide, the destination pages.

## Sequencing recommendation

1. Safety first. It needs only official advisories, which are free and dated, and it has the highest search intent.
2. Costs second. Fees and visa come from official sites; leave out bands that can't be sourced.
3. Cruises last, once at least one cruise partner is live. Until then, consider the qualitative "How to Choose a Nile
   Cruise".
